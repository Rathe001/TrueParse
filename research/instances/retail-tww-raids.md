# The War Within — raids

- **Type:** 3 raids, 24 bosses. **Boss-only**, thin treatment.
- **Sources:** Skycoach for Nerub-ar Palace and Manaforge Omega,
  ConquestCapped for Liberation of Undermine, read 2026-09-08. Single source
  each — Icy Veins' raid pages for all three are hubs that link per-boss
  guides and carry no mechanics themselves.
- **Status:** drafted

---

## Nerub-ar Palace (11.0)

Ulgrax the Devourer → The Bloodbound Horror → Sikran, Captain of the Sureki →
Rasha'nan → Broodtwister Ovi'nax → Nexus-Princess Ky'veza → The Silken Court →
Queen Ansurek.

**Order note:** linear except after Rasha'nan, where Broodtwister Ovi'nax and
Nexus-Princess Ky'veza can be taken in either order before The Silken Court.
Another non-fixed sequence, like The Venomous Abyss' wings.

**Ulgrax the Devourer**
1. Stalker Netting roots you — break out or Disembowel stacks finish you
2. Kill the adds before they pile up through the Insatiable Rage energy phases
- `TANK` tank — "swap on Tenderized" · ability `Tenderized`

**The Bloodbound Horror**
1. Phase 2 spawns adds that hit hard — tank them off the raid
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Baneful Shift, but the handling changes by difficulty" · ability `Baneful Shift`

**Sikran, Captain of the Sureki**
1. His weapon decides the fight — dual swords and polearm are different ability sets
2. Surekian Tethers chain damage: watch where you stand

**Rasha'nan**
1. Kill the Sticky Webs before they become Acid Pools
2. Rolling Acid knocks you back — position for it

**Broodtwister Ovi'nax**
1. CC the Blood Parasites, and control which eggs hatch
- `TANK` tank — "Volatile Concoction scales with your missing health: stay above 75%" · ability `Volatile Concoction`

*Name conflict: Skycoach calls him "Eggtender Ovi'nax", Icy Veins
"Broodtwister Ovi'nax". Confirm against the journal.*

**Nexus-Princess Ky'veza**
1. Reaper executes low-health players — the raid stays topped
2. Stay out of the Nether Rifts or you take Atomized
3. Kill the Nether Phantoms in the Assassination phase

**The Silken Court**
1. Two bosses with conflicting mechanics across three phases
2. Kill the Devoted Worshippers or Cosmic Resonance goes off

**Queen Ansurek**
1. Three phases: poison, then adds, then the corruption phase
2. Mitigate the Liquefy burst
- `TANK` tank — "Feast cuts healing received hard — swap on it" · ability `Feast`

## Liberation of Undermine (11.1)

Vexie and the Geargrinders → then four in any order (Cauldron of Carnage,
Rik Reverb, Stix Bunkjunker, Sprocketmonger Lockenstock) → One-Armed Bandit →
Mug'Zee, Heads of Security → Chrome King Gallywix.

**Order note:** Vexie is fixed first, the last three are fixed, the middle
four are free. A third non-fixed sequence.

**Vexie and the Geargrinders**
1. Time the bike collisions to strip the plating
2. The oil slicks slide you around, and Bomb Voyage! ignites them
- `TANK` tank — "swap on the tank-buster stacks" · ability `Bomb Voyage!`

**Cauldron of Carnage**
1. Split into two teams — the bosses empower each other if they meet
2. Blistering Spite and Galvanized Spite only reset when you swap teams

**Rik Reverb**
1. Don't let the Amplifiers reach full energy and go Haywire
2. Blaring Drop covers the whole arena
- `TANK` tank — "swap around five stacks of Tinnitus" · ability `Tinnitus`

**Stix Bunkjunker**
1. Every bomb must be deactivated — a live one is the wipe
2. Kill the Territorial Bombshells inside 30 seconds
3. Trash Compactor knocks you into the hazards

**Sprocketmonger Lockenstock**
1. The conveyor traps and holes kill outright
2. Stagger the mine detonations so the debuffs do not overlap

**One-Armed Bandit**
1. Never repeat a token combination — a repeat is an instant wipe
2. Intercept Beam goes to unmarked players

**Mug'Zee, Heads of Security**
1. Every Cluster Bombs zone needs a soak
2. Ice Spears must hit the barriers, not players
- `TANK` tank — "the heavy phases need a swap" · ability `Cluster Bombs`

**Chrome King Gallywix**
1. Soak the Tick-Tock Canisters
2. Sabotage the Giga Coil consoles with charged bombs on a coordinated Giga Blast
3. Phase 3 overlaps bomb barrages with rocket volleys
- `TANK` tank — "the colossal bomb in the last phase needs a swap" · ability `Bigger Badder Bomb Blast`

## Manaforge Omega (11.2)

Plexus Sentinel → Loom'ithar → Soulbinder Naazindhri → Forgeweaver Araz →
The Soul Hunters (optional) → Fractillus → Nexus-King Salhadaar →
Dimensius, the All-Devouring.

**Prerequisite worth a `TASK` line:** every raider needs the K'aresh campaign
done and the **Reshii Wraps** equipped — the Phase Blink they grant is
mandatory, not optional, from the first boss onward.

**Plexus Sentinel**
1. Phase 2: Energy Cutters flood the room — Phase Blink through them
2. Avoid the Displacement Matrices
- `TANK` tank — "Obliteration Arcanocannon leaves residue pools: swap and drop them clear" · ability `Obliteration Arcanocannon`

**Loom'ithar**
1. Manage the Infusion Tethers — they dictate where you can stand
2. At 50% he splits, and the burst needs defensives
- `KICK` need kick — "Hyper Infusion" · ability `Hyper Infusion`
- (no role) — "Group up to soak Writhing Wave" · ability `Writhing Wave`

**Soulbinder Naazindhri**
1. Soulfray Annihilation orbs destroy whoever they reach
2. Kill the Unbound Souls before the Soulweave Chrysalis makes Ethereals
- `KICK` need kick — "the Binding Machines' casts, on a chain" · ability `Soulweave Chrysalis`

**Forgeweaver Araz**
1. Place the Arcane Echoes precisely — the arena runs out of room
2. The Void phase escalates raid damage the longer it runs
- `KICK` need kick — "Silencing Tempest" · ability `Silencing Tempest`

**The Soul Hunters** — optional
1. Three bosses, one void, one fire, one soul — the overlaps are the fight

**Fractillus**
1. The crystal walls march toward him lane by lane — break them in order
2. The shockwave knockbacks punish bad footing

**Nexus-King Salhadaar**
1. The Oath mechanics are individual checks — yours is yours to answer
2. Kill the elites through the intermissions
3. Three phases, and Dark Star interactions escalate through them

**Dimensius, the All-Devouring** — final
1. Devour kills anyone not positioned in the Excess Mass
2. The gravity mechanics never stop — position continuously
