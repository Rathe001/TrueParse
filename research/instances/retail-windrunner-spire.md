# Windrunner Spire — retail (Midnight)

- **Journal name:** Windrunner Spire
- **Type:** dungeon, 4 bosses
- **Pool:** Midnight Season 1 keystone pool; not in Season 2
- **Sources:** Icy Veins *Windrunner Spire Dungeon Guide* and Method
  *Windrunner Spire Mythic+ Dungeon Guide*, both read 2026-09-08. Reconciled;
  disagreements are recorded under CONFLICTS and no line was written from them.
- **Status:** drafted

Boss order: Emberdawn → Derelict Duo (Kalis & Latch) → Commander Kroluk →
The Restless Heart.

---

## Emberdawn

**core**
1. Flaming Updraft: drop it at the arena edge, away from the group
2. Fire Breath sweeps four frontals counter-clockwise from the tank — rotate with it
3. Dodge the twisters through the Burning Gale intermission

**notes**
- `CD` healer — "the Burning Gale intermission" · ability `Burning Gale` · *both*
- `TANK` tank — "Searing Beak: a defensive that covers the DoT too" · ability `Searing Beak` · *both*

No lust call in either source.

## Derelict Duo (Kalis & Latch)

**core**
1. Cleave both evenly: Broken Bond empowers whichever one is left alive
2. Heaving Yank: the hooked player runs behind Latch
3. Splattering Spew: take it out to the perimeter

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "Kalis's Shadow Bolt, every cast" · ability `Shadow Bolt` · *both*
- `CURSE` need curse — "Curse of Darkness" · ability `Curse of Darkness` · *both*
- `TANK` tank — "Bone Hack: defensive" · ability `Bone Hack` · *both*

## Commander Kroluk

**core**
1. Rallying Bellow at 66 and 33: the adds die before the boss takes damage again
2. Reckless Leap hits the furthest player — dodge the falling rubble
3. Stack for Intimidating Shout

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "Chain Lightning from the Phantasmal Mystics, every cast" · ability `Chain Lightning` · *both*
- `TANK` tank — "Rampage: defensive" · ability `Rampage` · *both*
- `CD` healer — "the Rallying Bellow add phases at 66 and 33" · ability `Rallying Bellow` · *both*
- (no role) — "Bladestorm fixates: kite it away" · ability `Bladestorm` · *both*

## The Restless Heart

**core**
1. Clear Squall Leap stacks by standing on the Turbulent Arrows
2. Be on an arrow when Bullseye Windblast goes off
3. Spread for Gust Shot — it clears the puddles you are standing in

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "Tempest Slash: put an arrow at your back, it cancels the knockback and the stacks" · ability `Tempest Slash` · *Method; IV has only "active mitigation"*
- `CD` healer — "watch Squall Leap stacks" · ability `Squall Leap` · *both*

## CONFLICTS — not written, need a third source or in-game check

- **Bolt Gale (The Restless Heart).** Icy Veins calls it interruptible; Method
  describes it as a frontal channel you stand still for and mitigate. A kick
  line is worth a lot to a DPS reader, so this is worth resolving.
- **Intimidating Shout.** Icy Veins lists it on both the Derelict Duo and
  Commander Kroluk; Method lists it only on Kroluk. Written on Kroluk only.
- **Bullseye Windblast.** Method calls it a frontal; Icy Veins' overview calls
  it a growing circle that stuns. Both agree the answer is to be pre-positioned
  on an arrow, so the line is written on the action, not the shape.
- **Lust.** Neither source makes a call for this dungeon. Left out rather than
  invented.
