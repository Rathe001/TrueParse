# Heart of Fear — MoP Classic

- **Journal name:** Heart of Fear
- **Type:** raid, 6 bosses. **Boss-only** — no trash, per Josh 2026-09-08.
- **Source:** Icy Veins MoP Classic per-boss encounter guides, read 2026-09-08.
  Single source; second-source pass still owed.
- **Status:** drafted, needs a second source

Boss order: Imperial Vizier Zor'lok → Blade Lord Ta'yak → Garalon →
Wind Lord Mel'jarak → Amber-Shaper Un'sok → Grand Empress Shek'zeer.

---

## 1. Imperial Vizier Zor'lok

**core**
1. Somebody stays in melee of him and of every Echo, or Song of the Empress wipes the raid
2. Force and Verve: get inside a shield ring for the 60% reduction
3. Convert mind-controls two to five of you — damage them to 50% to break it

**notes**
- `LUST` need lust — "the phase 2 opener at 40%" · ability `Force and Verve`
- `TANK` tank — "Intercept the Exhale beam off whoever it picked" · ability `Exhale`
- `CD` healer — "Force and Verve, and keep instants rolling through Attenuation" · ability `Attenuation`
- (no role) — "Stay off the centre until phase 2: Pheromones of Zeal fills it" · ability `Pheromones of Zeal`

---

## 2. Blade Lord Ta'yak

**core**
1. Unseen Strike: the whole raid stacks on the marked player to split it
2. Keep clear of the tornadoes as they pile up — they last the whole phase
3. Phase 2 at 20%: keep moving through Storm Unleashed

**notes**
- `LUST` need lust — "on the pull" · *the guide is explicit: 40 seconds before the first major ability*
- `TANK` tank — "Overwhelming Assault: swap every two stacks, defensive at one" · ability `Overwhelming Assault`
- `CD` healer — "every Unseen Strike, and all of phase 2" · ability `Unseen Strike`
- (no role) min `h` — "Blade Tempest pulls the whole raid onto him: get out inside two seconds" · ability `Blade Tempest`

---

## 3. Garalon

**core**
1. Kill the legs from inside the Weak Point circles — each broken leg costs him health and speed
2. Never walk under him: entering his hitbox triggers Crush immediately
3. Pass Pheromones on at 12-14 stacks, and kite it round the outside

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "Stand in front with at least one other player — Furious Swipe hitting fewer than two gives him a Fury stack" · ability `Furious Swipe`
- `CD` healer — "every Crush" · ability `Crush`
- (no role) min `h` — "From 33% he stops chasing the Pheromones target and has to be tanked" · ability `Pheromones`

---

## 4. Wind Lord Mel'jarak

**core**
1. Kill the add groups first — he takes 99% more damage once they are all down
2. Never touch an armed Wind Bomb
3. Spread for Whirling Blade: it hits on the way out and again coming back

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "Mending from the Zar'thik Battle-Menders, without exception" · ability `Mending`
- `PURGE` need purge — "Quickening — it gives every mantid 35% damage and attack speed" · ability `Quickening`
- `LUST` need lust — "a Recklessness window once the adds are down" · ability `Recklessness`
- `TANK` tank — "Keep the active adds away from the ones you have CC'd; defensive through Recklessness" · ability `Recklessness`
- `CD` healer — "Rain of Blades, and the triple Kor'thik Strike on one player" · ability `Rain of Blades`

---

## 5. Amber-Shaper Un'sok

**core**
1. Reshape Life turns players into Mutated Constructs — use Amber Strike to stack Destabilize on the boss
2. Amber Explosion from a Construct or the Monstrosity kills the raid: interrupt it with Amber Strike
3. Spread 8 yards so the Living Ambers do not chain their explosions

**notes**
- `LUST` need lust — "phase 3" · ability `Volatile Amber`
- `TANK` tank — "Alternate the Construct duty, and eat Burning Amber pools to keep Willpower up" · ability `Reshape Life`
- `CD` healer — "Parasitic Growth: heal it as little as you can — healing makes it hit harder. Absorbs do not feed it" · ability `Parasitic Growth`

---

## 6. Grand Empress Shek'zeer

**core**
1. Leave a Dissonance Field before Sonic Discharge blows it
2. Combine the Sticky Resin pools — five combined makes the Amber Trap you need for the Reavers
3. Phase 3 under 30%: everything lands at once, stay ahead of Calamity

**notes**
- `LUST` need lust — "the moment phase 3 starts" · ability `Amassing Darkness`
- `KICK` need kick — "Dispatch from the Set'thik Windblades" · ability `Dispatch`
- `FEAR` **new capability** — "the phase 3 fears: Visions of Demise marks players" · ability `Visions of Demise`
- `TANK` tank — "Eyes of the Empress: swap at three or four — five mind-controls you" · ability `Eyes of the Empress`
- `CD` healer — "phase 3 is continuous: rotate everything" · ability `Amassing Darkness`
- (no role) — "Don't kill the Set'thik Windblades until two Amber Traps exist" · ability `Sticky Resin`

**Capability note.** The guide tells healers to *Mass Dispel* the phase 3 fears.
By the rule established on Mogu'shan Vaults, that does **not** make this a
`MASSDISPEL` line — Mass Dispel is the efficient tool here, not the only one.
It is a fear that any fear answer can address, so it takes the new `FEAR` key.
