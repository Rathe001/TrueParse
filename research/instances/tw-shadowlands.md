# Timewalking pool — Shadowlands

- **Type:** 6 legacy dungeons, **thin treatment** (Josh 2026-09-08): bosses and
  the two or three lines that still kill people. No trash.
- **Source:** Icy Veins per-dungeon guides, read 2026-09-08. Single source —
  these are stable, long-settled encounters, but the second-source pass is
  still owed if any of this is to ship.
- **Status:** drafted

**File convention note.** Legacy is grouped one file per Timewalking pool
rather than one per instance. At thin depth a per-instance file is mostly
header, and the pool is the unit players actually meet.

## Halls of Atonement

Halkias, the Sin-Stained Goliath → Echelon → High Adjudicator Aleez →
Lord Chamberlain.

**Halkias, the Sin-Stained Goliath**
1. Dodge the rotating Refracted Sinlight cross — it changes direction
2. Don't stand near the tank for Crumbling Slam
- `FEAR` need fear — "Sinlight Visions, straight away — it lands on a DPS" · ability `Sinlight Visions`

**Echelon**
1. Stone Call: gather the adds and kill them together, or they get raised again
2. The marked player takes Stone Shattering Leap onto the corpses to destroy them
- `KICK` need kick, min `m` — "Villainous Bolt from the adds" · ability `Villainous Bolt`

**High Adjudicator Aleez**
1. Stay away from whoever the Pulse from Beyond add is fixating
2. Dodge the Anima Fountain swirls
- `KICK` need kick — "Volley of Power before Bolt of Power — that is the priority" · ability `Volley of Power`

**Lord Chamberlain**
1. Telekinetic Toss: dodge the statue the red arrow points at
2. Ritual of Woe: the tank intercepts the beam
- `CD` healer — "Stigma of Pride ramps on the tank for 18 seconds" · ability `Stigma of Pride`

## De Other Side

Hakkar the Soulflayer → The Manastorms → Dealer Xy'exa → Mueh'zala.

**Hakkar the Soulflayer**
1. Corrupted Blood spreads — get away from everyone until it drops
2. Kill the Sons of Hakkar fast, and move off the pool each one leaves
- `KICK` need kick — "Blood Barrage, once the shield breaks" · ability `Blood Barrage`

**The Manastorms** (Millhouse and Millificent)
1. Intercept the purple crystal beams for the stacking damage buff
2. Right-click the walking Experimental Squirrel Bombs to disarm them
- `KICK` need kick — "Frostbolt" · ability `Frostbolt`

**Dealer Xy'exa**
1. Dodge the white Displacement Traps — but you need one alive for the bomb
2. Explosive Contrivance: step on a trap as the timer ends to contain it

**Mueh'zala**
1. Master of Death: read his arms and dodge left, right or back — three times
2. Portal phase: kill the adds, then click the totem for Bwonsamdi's buff
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Cosmic Artifice: dispel one, let the other expire" · ability `Cosmic Artifice`

## The Necrotic Wake

Blightbone → Amarth, The Harvester → Surgeon Stitchflesh →
Nalthor the Rimebinder.

**Blightbone**
1. Stay out of the Fetid Gas
2. Lock down the Carrion Worms before Blood Gorge stacks up
- `CD` healer — "Heaving Retch" · ability `Heaving Retch`

**Amarth, The Harvester**
1. Kill the Land of the Dead adds before Final Harvest, or the party wipes
2. Dodge the Necrotic Breath frontal
- `KICK` need kick — "Necrotic Bolt" · ability `Necrotic Bolt`
- `SOOTHE` need soothe — "Unholy Frenzy on the adds" · ability `Unholy Frenzy`

**Surgeon Stitchflesh**
1. Morbid Fixation: run, or reset it outright
2. Kill the adds so Festering Rot cannot stack on the group
- `TANK` tank — "Stitchneedle bleed: defensive" · ability `Stitchneedle`

**Nalthor the Rimebinder**
1. Dark Exile: kill the Zolramus Siphoner in there or you die in there
2. Icebound Aegis: healing cooldowns
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Frozen Binds, when it is safe to" · ability `Frozen Binds`
- `TANK` tank — "Icy Shard: active mitigation" · ability `Icy Shard`

## Plaguefall

Globgrog → Doctor Ickus → Domina Venomblade → Margrave Stradama.

**Globgrog**
1. Kill the slimes before they reach him — they heal him
2. Mind the knockback near the platform edge
- `DISEASE` need disease — "Debilitating Plague" · ability `Debilitating Plague`

**Doctor Ickus**
1. Dodge the Slime Lunge leap
2. Get clear of Plague Bomb
- `KICK` need kick — "Viral Globs" · ability `Viral Globs`

**Domina Venomblade**
1. Shadow Ambush: get away from everyone else
2. Stay grouped or the Brood Assassins stun you (Mythic)
- `POISON` need poison — "Cytotoxic Slash" · ability `Cytotoxic Slash`

**Margrave Stradama**
1. Dodge the Plague Crash cones from the tentacles
2. Stay out of the green pool
- `TANK` tank — "Touch of Slime: soak it" · ability `Touch of Slime`

## Sanguine Depths

Kryxis the Voracious → Executor Tarvold → Grand Proctor Beryllia →
General Kaal.

**Kryxis the Voracious**
1. Soak the orbs or he empowers himself
2. Mind the knockback near the edges
- `KICK` need kick — "the Hungering Drain channel" · ability `Hungering Drain`

**Executor Tarvold**
1. Spread for the targeted circles
2. Stay out of the toxic pools the adds leave behind

**Grand Proctor Beryllia**
1. Stand in the mitigation zones
2. Dodge the swirls

**General Kaal**
1. Dodge the charges — the bleed stacks if you are in close
2. Move for the knockback waves
- `CD` healer — "her ultimate: this is what the external action button is for" · ability `Glyph of Destruction`

## Spires of Ascension

Kin-Tara → Ventunax → Oryphrion → Devos, Paragon of Doubt.

**Kin-Tara**
1. Dodge Charged Spear in the flight phase
2. Don't stand in front of her
- `KICK` need kick — "Dark Lance, as often as it comes" · ability `Dark Lance`

**Ventunax**
1. Move away from the Shadowhirl clouds and dodge the bolts they throw
2. Dodge the Blinding Flash frontal

**Oryphrion**
1. Spread 8 yards for Purifying Blast
2. Recharge Anima: soak the blue orbs before they reach him
3. Leave the Volatile Anima circle before the debuff expires
- `MAGIC` need magic — "the tank's Charged Stomp" · ability `Charged Stomp`

**Devos, Paragon of Doubt**
1. Dodge Run Through
2. Conduit phase: collect the orbs against the wind, then throw the spear back
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Lingering Doubt, and stay out of the pool it drops" · ability `Lingering Doubt`
