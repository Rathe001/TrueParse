# Timewalking raids

- **Type:** 4 legacy raids, **thin treatment**, boss-only (raids carry no trash
  by decision).
- **Sources:** Method for Blackrock Depths; wow.gg for Black Temple; Overgear
  for Firelands; wowtbc.gg for Ulduar. All read 2026-09-08. Single source
  each. Warcraft Tavern was the first Ulduar source and was discarded as
  unusable — it described every mechanic as "dispellable" or "interruptible"
  without naming one spell or school.
- **Status:** drafted

These are the only legacy raids with a live max-level audience, which is why
they come before the other ~46.

---

## Black Temple — Burning Crusade

High Warlord Naj'entus → Supremus → Shade of Akama → Teron Gorefiend →
Gurtogg Bloodboil → Reliquary of Souls → Mother Shahraz → Illidari Council →
Illidan Stormrage.

**High Warlord Naj'entus**
1. Tidal Shield makes him invulnerable and heals him — free the impaled player and throw the Naj'entus Spine at him to break it
2. Be at full health for Tidal Burst

**Supremus**
1. During the Fixate phase, run — he kills whoever he is chasing if they stop

**Shade of Akama**
1. Burn the Shade before it kills Akama
- `KICK` need kick — "Chain Heal from the Spiritbinders" · ability `Chain Heal`

**Teron Gorefiend**
1. Shadow of Death kills its target every 30 seconds — they come back as a ghost and have to kill their own Constructs
2. Constructs that reach the living raid are what wipe it
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Incinerate" · ability `Incinerate`

**Gurtogg Bloodboil**
1. The Fel Rage target dies if the tank loses them

**Reliquary of Souls**
1. Phase 2 reflects your damage — stop attacking
2. Phase 3 raid damage escalates to the end
- `KICK` need kick — "Spirit Shock, every cast" · ability `Spirit Shock`
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Soul Drain, immediately" · ability `Soul Drain`
- `PURGE` need purge — "Rune Shield — Spellsteal it" · ability `Rune Shield`

**Mother Shahraz**
1. Fatal Attraction bonds players — run apart or it kills them
2. Saber Lash needs a tank rotation, one tank cannot hold it

**Illidari Council** (Gathios the Shatterer, High Nethermancer Zerevor,
Veras Darkshadow, Lady Malande)
1. All four must die within moments of each other
- `KICK` need kick — "Circle of Healing, every single cast, or the fight resets" · ability `Circle of Healing`
- `POISON` need poison — "Veras' Deadly Poison" · ability `Deadly Poison`

**Illidan Stormrage**
1. Shear cuts 60% of the tank's max health and cannot miss — it has to be blocked, dodged or parried
2. Shadow Demons stun and then kill — break them off
3. Stay 15 yards clear of Aura of Dread
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Parasitic Shadowfiend" · ability `Parasitic Shadowfiend`

## Firelands — Cataclysm

**The first four are open in any order**: Shannox, Lord Rhyolith, Beth'tilac,
Alysrazor. Then Baleroc → Majordomo Staghelm → Ragnaros.

**Shannox**
1. Hurl Spear and Magma Rupture kill outright if you stand in the marked ground
2. Jagged Tear stacks on whoever the hounds are on

**Lord Rhyolith**
1. Steer him onto the erupting volcanoes — you do not attack him directly through the armour
2. Superheated stacks in phase two are the clock

**Beth'tilac**
1. Phase two: The Widow's Kiss forces a tank swap
2. Burning Acid from the adds escalates

**Alysrazor**
1. Never touch the adds — Gushing Wound bleeds you out fast
2. Full Power needs defensives, not movement

**Baleroc**
1. Inferno Blade and Decimation Blade are the two killers — know which is coming
2. Torment spreads through the raid; the healers build Vital Spark stacks to keep up

**Majordomo Staghelm**
1. Move the instant Leaping Flames lands
2. Burning Orbs keep damaging after the soak ends

**Ragnaros**
1. Three stages and two intermissions — Lava Waves and Living Meteors are what kill

## Blackrock Depths (Timewalking) — Classic

Added in 11.0.5 for the 20th anniversary; 10-15 players, LFR / Normal /
Heroic, and Raid Finder only in the 2025 return.

Lord Roccor → Bael'gar → Lord Incendius → Golem Lord Argelmach → The Seven →
General Angerforge → Ambassador Flamelash → Emperor Dagran Thaurissan.

**Lord Roccor**
- `KICK` need kick — "Slag Armor on the Sons of Roccor" · ability `Slag Armor`
- `CD` healer — "the Volcanic Upheaval channel" · ability `Volcanic Upheaval`

**Bael'gar**
1. Get behind a boulder for Molten Furnace or it kills you
2. Shatter hits harder for every boulder still standing

**Lord Incendius**
1. Somebody stands in the Dying Flare circle — unsoaked it kills
2. Kill the Flame Geysers on sight
- `KICK` need kick — "Blaze" · ability `Blaze`
- `POISON` need poison — "the adds' poison" · ability `Poison`

**Golem Lord Argelmach**
1. Intercept the Power Generator orb to cut his damage
2. Lethal Attraction links two players — stay inside 5 yards of each other
- `POISON` need poison — "Poison Mist" · ability `Poison Mist`
- *Method's page spells him "Arglemach"; the long-standing spelling is Argelmach — confirm against the journal*

**The Seven**
1. Kill the summoned Fire Elemental immediately
- `KICK` need kick — "Shadowbolt Volley, and the Summon Felguard" · ability `Summon Felguard`
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Magma Burn, and the Banish" · ability `Magma Burn`

**General Angerforge**
1. Kill the Officer first — its Commanding Aura makes everything else uninterruptible
2. Dodge the Artillery Barrage swirls, and line-of-sight the Fireburst Grenade

**Ambassador Flamelash**
1. Kill the Portal Anchors or the portals keep feeding his damage
2. Flamewalkers explode on death
- `TANK` tank — "the Cremate channel" · ability `Cremate`

**Emperor Dagran Thaurissan**
1. Volcanic Blast from the Avatar of Ragnaros is shared damage — spread the load
- `KICK` need kick — "Moment of Twilight, or it wipes the raid; and Mind Blast" · ability `Moment of Twilight`
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Necrosis, and Shadow Word: Nemesis before it ramps" · ability `Shadow Word: Nemesis`

## Ulduar — Wrath of the Lich King

Flame Leviathan → Ignis the Furnace Master → Razorscale → XT-002
Deconstructor → Assembly of Iron → Kologarn → Auriaya → Hodir → Thorim →
Freya → Mimiron → General Vezax → Yogg-Saron → Algalon the Observer.

Fourteen bosses, several optional. Re-sourced from wowtbc.gg after the first
source proved unusable (it said "dispellable" without naming a school or a
spell anywhere).

**Flame Leviathan**
1. Fought entirely in vehicles — Siege Engines, Demolishers, Choppers
2. Pursued fixates a vehicle: keep moving

**Ignis the Furnace Master**
1. The Slag Pot grabs a player, who can still cast while inside it
2. The Iron Constructs are the fight — shatter them brittle

**Razorscale**
1. Harpoons are what ground her; Flame Breath through the transitions is the killer
- `TANK` tank — "Fuse Armor: taunt swap on stacks" · ability `Fuse Armor`

**XT-002 Deconstructor**
1. Searing Light and Gravity Bomb target players — take them away from the group
2. Kill the Scrapbots before they heal him
- `CD` healer — "Tympanic Tantrum" · ability `Tympanic Tantrum`

**Assembly of Iron**
1. Brundir's Overload has to be run from
- `KICK` need kick — "Chain Lightning" · ability `Chain Lightning`
- (dispel, school not stated) — "Fusion Punch" — *the source calls it "Physical", which is not a dispel school; no tag written*

**Kologarn**
1. Stone Grip picks players up — free them or they die
2. Kill the Rubble adds
- `TANK` tank — "Overhead Smash: taunt swap on stacks" · ability `Overhead Smash`

**Auriaya**
1. Sonic Screech is the raid hit; the Feral Defender has to be handled every time it revives
- `KICK` need kick — "Sentinel Blast" · ability `Sentinel Blast`
- `FEAR` need fear — "Terrifying Screech" · ability `Terrifying Screech`

**Hodir**
1. Flash Freeze stuns and kills whoever is caught out — get behind cover
2. Biting Cold stacks the longer you stand still, and the Icicles fall on you
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Freeze" · ability `Freeze`

**Thorim**
1. The raid splits: half runs the gauntlet to him, half holds the arena
2. Lightning Charge stacks into an enrage — the gauntlet has a clock
- `KICK` need kick — "Runic Lightning, and Runic Mending" · ability `Runic Mending`

**Freya**
1. Kill the Detonating Lashers before they reach anyone
2. Conservator's Grip silences — break the Conservator's roots
3. Nature Bomb lands where you are standing

**Mimiron**
1. Rocket Strike kills outright — never stand in the marked line
2. Each phase transition changes the fight completely
- `TANK` tank — "Plasma Blast" · ability `Plasma Blast`

**General Vezax**
1. Shadow Crash comes down a line — get off it (and the pools it leaves are the mana source)
2. Surge of Darkness has to be kited
- `KICK` need kick — "Searing Flames" · ability `Searing Flames`
- (no role) — "Mark of the Faceless heals him off the target: break line of sight" · ability `Mark of the Faceless`

**Yogg-Saron**
1. Sanity is the real health bar — Lunatic Gaze drains it and mind-controls at zero
2. The tentacles and the Immortal Guardian phase are what end pulls
- `KICK` need kick — "Dark Volley" · ability `Dark Volley`

**Algalon the Observer**
1. Big Bang kills everyone not inside a Black Hole
2. Cosmic Smash drops zones across the room
- `TANK` tank — "Quantum Strike" · ability `Quantum Strike`

## Sourcing quality across these four

Blackrock Depths is the best sourced (Method, per-boss, with named interrupts
and dispels), Black Temple close behind, and Ulduar is now solid after
re-sourcing. Firelands is the thinnest — adequate for the thin treatment, but
its source names no interrupts or dispels at all for any of the seven bosses,
which is plausible for Firelands but worth a second look.

**One conflict left unwritten:** Assembly of Iron's Fusion Punch is called a
"Physical" dispel, which is not a thing. Either it is a magic dispel or the
source is wrong; no line written until that is settled.

**A fifth `FEAR` case:** Auriaya's Terrifying Screech.
