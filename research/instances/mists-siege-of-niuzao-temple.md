Boss order: Vizier Jin'bak -> Commander Vo'jak -> General Pa'valak ->
Wing Leader Ner'onok.

## 1. Vizier Jin'bak

**core**
1. Kill the Sap Globules before they reach the middle - Detonate hits as hard as the puddle got big

## 2. Commander Vo'jak

**core**
1. Use the Caustic Tar jars on the incoming waves, and again to strip his Rising Speed
2. Single-target the Sik'thik Demolishers and stay spread - they explode on death
3. Get off the Dashing Strike path before Thousand Blades follows it

**notes**
- `TANK` tank - "Dodge his cleave rather than eat it" - ability `Thousand Blades`

## 3. General Pa'valak

**core**
1. Take the Throw Explosives from the Amber-Sappers and throw them back through the shield phases at 65% and 35%
2. Dodge the Blade Rush: he throws the sword first, then charges where it landed

**notes**
- `TANK` tank - "Kite him: standing and trading is what kills tanks here" - ability `Blade Rush`

## 4. Wing Leader Ner'onok

**core**
1. Keep jumping to hold the Amber Resin bar down, or it stuns you for 10 seconds
2. At 66% and 33% push through Gusting Winds to reach him and break the channel
3. Stay out of the Caustic Pitch pools

**notes**
- `TANK` tank - "Face him so the cleave misses the group, and so the resin lands where you want it" - ability `Caustic Pitch`

## Second source, 2026-09-08

**Confirms:** Sap Globules feeding the central puddle and Detonate scaling
with it; the Caustic Tar jars slowing the waves; killing the Sik'thik
Demolishers first because they explode.

**Adds:** the wave composition is Amberwing, Swarmer and Demolisher.

**Deliberately not written:** the second source's Residue/Resin Flakes
pre-stacking trick is a Challenge Mode speed tactic, not something a group
needs told mid-run.
