# Timewalking pool — Cataclysm

- **Type:** 6 legacy dungeons, **thin treatment**: bosses and the two or three
  lines that still kill people. No trash.
- **Sources:** Icy Veins *Cataclysm Classic* per-dungeon guides, read
  2026-09-08 — a clean, complete set, much better sourcing than the Warlords
  pool. Single source.
- **Status:** drafted

---

## Blackrock Caverns

Rom'ogg Bonecrusher → Corla, Herald of Twilight → Karsh Steelbender →
Beauty → Ascendant Lord Obsidius.

**Rom'ogg Bonecrusher**
1. Quake drops a dust cloud under everyone — move before they go off
2. The Skullcracker kills anyone still in melee
3. Chains of Woe at 66% and 33% pulls the group in and roots it

**Corla, Herald of Twilight**
1. Soak the beams in shifts — Evolution at 100 turns you hostile
- `FEAR` need fear — "Dark Command: the fear pulls soakers off their beam" · ability `Dark Command`

**Karsh Steelbender**
1. Superheated stacks the longer you stay in the flame — rotate through it

**Beauty**
1. **Never kill Runty.** She frenzies and berserks the party if you do
2. Magma Spit leaves a splash that explodes on anyone near it
- `FEAR` need fear — "Terrifying Roar, or somebody gets feared into Runty" · ability `Terrifying Roar`

**Ascendant Lord Obsidius**
1. The Shadows of Obsidius chase whoever last hit them — hand them off deliberately
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Thunderclap, off the kiter" · ability `Thunderclap`

## End Time

**Two of the four shrine bosses are chosen at random**, then Murozond. Worth
noting for the tracker: this dungeon's boss list is not fixed run to run.

**Echo of Jaina**
1. Dodge the Flarecore mines — they escalate as they detonate
2. Frost Blades stuns for 5 seconds
- `KICK` need kick — "Frostbolt Volley" · ability `Frostbolt Volley`

**Echo of Baine**
1. Pulverize destroys platforms every 50 seconds — keep ahead of it
2. Pick the thrown totem up and throw it back: it raises his damage taken

**Echo of Sylvanas**
1. The ghoul circle closes in — focus one ghoul to break out
2. Never cross the beam between them, it kills quickly

**Echo of Tyrande**
1. Piercing Gaze of Elune silences casters
- `KICK` need kick — "Stardust, top priority — Lunar Guidance keeps making it faster" · ability `Stardust`

**Murozond** — final
1. The hourglass has five charges: use one to clear the Distortion Bombs and your Temporal Blast stacks

## Lost City of the Tol'vir

General Husam → Lockmaw and Augh → High Prophet Barim → Siamat.

**General Husam**
1. Mystic Traps arm after 3 seconds and go off on contact
2. Dodge the Shockwave X-pattern, and the Bad Intentions charge

**Lockmaw and Augh**
1. Scent of Blood spawns Frenzied Crocolisks that fixate the marked player
2. Don't stand behind him for Dust Flail
- `DISEASE` need disease — "Viscous Poison" · ability `Viscous Poison`
- `POISON` need poison — "Paralytic Blow Dart" · ability `Paralytic Blow Dart`

**High Prophet Barim**
1. Heaven's Fury beams a random player — move out from under it
2. Phase 2: stop the Soul Fragments reaching the Harbinger, they feed its damage
- `DISEASE` need disease — "Plague of Ages, fast — it bounces" · ability `Plague of Ages`

**Siamat**
1. Cloud Burst knocks back and leaves damage zones
2. Wailing Winds throws you around the room — position before it starts
3. Take Thunder Crash away from the group

## The Stonecore

Corborus → Slabhide → Ozruk → High Priestess Azil.

**Corborus**
1. Spread for Crystal Barrage and kill the shards it spawns
2. Get out of the Thrashing Charge dust clouds or die

**Slabhide**
1. Move off a Lava Fissure — it erupts after 5 seconds
2. Crystal Storm: line-of-sight it behind a Stalactite pillar

**Ozruk**
1. Never stand in front of Ground Slam — it is fatal
2. Run away the moment Shatter starts
3. Paralyze is an 8-second stun with heavy arcane damage on top

**High Priestess Azil**
1. Watch the ground for Seismic Shards and move
2. Get clear of the Gravity Wells before they form
- `KICK` need kick — "Force Grip, it is massive tank damage" · ability `Force Grip`

## The Vortex Pinnacle

Grand Vizier Ertan → Altairus → Asaad.

**Grand Vizier Ertan**
1. Spread — the Cyclone Shield recall damages and slows everyone near him
2. Face the Lurking Tempests and they play dead

**Altairus**
1. Call the Wind decides everything: be **Upwind** of him, never downwind
2. Spread for Chilling Breath, and stay out of the Twisting Winds

**Asaad**
1. Jump just before Static Cling lands or you are rooted for 18 seconds
2. Get inside the Unstable Grounding Field triangle — Supremacy of the Storm kills everyone outside it
3. Ranged kill the Skyfall Stars

## Throne of the Tides

Lady Naz'jar → Commander Ulthok → Mindbender Ghur'sha → Ozumat.

**Lady Naz'jar**
1. Handle the adds through her waterspout immunity phases
2. Stay out of the Summon Geyser ground effects
- `KICK` need kick — "Shock Blast" · ability `Shock Blast`
- `DISEASE` need disease — "Fungal Spores" · ability `Fungal Spores`

**Commander Ulthok**
1. Kite away from the Dark Fissure void zones as they expand
2. Squeeze stuns — save a break for it

**Mindbender Ghur'sha**
1. Dodge the Earth Shards
2. Phase 2: damage the enslaved player to 50% to free the boss

**Ozumat**
1. AoE the Blight Beasts down in phase one, kite the slow ones in phase two
- `KICK` need kick — "Brain Spike from the Vicious Mindlashers — it burns mana" · ability `Brain Spike`

## Note

Blackrock Caverns produces two more `FEAR` cases — Corla's Dark Command and
Beauty's Terrifying Roar — and both are the kind where the fear itself is the
killer (it pulls a soaker off a beam, or drags someone into Runty). More
evidence that fear deserved its own capability key rather than being folded
into `MAGIC`.

**End Time has a randomised boss list** (two of four shrine bosses per run).
Like Eye of Azshara's free order, this does not fit a fixed boss sequence.
