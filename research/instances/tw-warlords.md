# Timewalking pool — Warlords of Draenor

- **Type:** 6 legacy dungeons, **thin treatment**: bosses and the two or three
  lines that still kill people. No trash.
- **Sources:** Icy Veins (Shadowmoon Burial Grounds) and Ten Ton Hammer's
  Warlords dungeon guides (the other five), read 2026-09-08. Skyreach has
  both. **Re-sourced 2026-09-08** — the first pass was built from search
  summaries and left four bosses with no mechanics at all; all four are filled
  in now from a named guide.
- **Status:** drafted

## Auchindoun

Vigilant Kaathar → Soulbinder Nyami → Azzakel → Teron'gor.

**Vigilant Kaathar**
1. Move off Hallowed Ground before it activates
2. Get behind the Holy Shield for Consecrated Light
3. Stay off the cracks Sanctified Strike leaves

**Soulbinder Nyami**
1. Soul Vessel is a channelled AoE — move to the centre
- `KICK` need kick — "Mind Spike, and the adds' Arbiter's Hammer and Arcane Bolt" · ability `Mind Spike`

**Azzakel**
1. Spread for Curtain of Flame — it burns one player for 9 seconds
2. Add wave: the tank takes the Felguards, DPS kill the Cackling Pyromaniacs, stay out of the Trickster pools
- `TANK` tank — "Fel Lash: defensive — it leaves you taking more damage for the next 6 seconds, and knocks you back" · ability `Fel Lash`
- `KICK` need kick — "Felblast from the Felguards" · ability `Felblast`

**Teron'gor**
1. Below 75% he takes one of three warlock specialisations at random — the emote tells you which
- `KICK` need kick — "Rain of Fire and Drain Life" · ability `Drain Life`
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Corruption, and the DoTs he keeps putting out" · ability `Corruption`

## Bloodmaul Slag Mines

Magmolatus → Slave Watcher Crushto → Roltall → Gug'rokk.

*Order conflict: Ten Ton Hammer puts Magmolatus first, the earlier search
sourcing put Slave Watcher Crushto first. Confirm against the journal.*

**Magmolatus** (phase 1 is Forgemaster Gog'duh, phase 2 is Magmolatus)
1. Phase 1: the tank holds the Ruination and Calamity elementals away; kill adds before the boss
2. Phase 2: kill the Molten Elementals fast, and the tank steps away from Slag Smash
- `KICK` need kick — "Calamity's Firestorm; then Magma Barrage and Volcanic Tantrum" · ability `Firestorm`
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Dancing Flames, and the slow in phase two" · ability `Dancing Flames`

**Slave Watcher Crushto**
1. Dodge the Earth Crush circles
2. Wild Slam knocks everyone back, then Crushing Leap lands on someone for an 8-second stun — it cannot be avoided, so mitigate it
- `KICK` need kick — "Ferocious Yell, or his damage climbs" · ability `Ferocious Yell`

**Roltall**
1. Three Fiery Boulders per wave, down random lanes
2. Burning Slag leaves permanent pools — the bridge runs out of room, so this is a soft enrage

**Gug'rokk**
1. Step out of the Magma Eruption pool under you at once
2. Kill the Unstable Slags before they reach the kilns
- `KICK` need kick — "Molten Blast, or Molten Core stacks on him" · ability `Molten Blast`
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Molten Blast if a cast gets through" · ability `Molten Core`

## The Everbloom

Witherbark → Ancient Protectors → Xeri'tac (optional) → Archmage Sol → Yalnu.

**Witherbark**
1. Kill the Aqueous Globules before they reach him — that is what extends the double-damage window
2. Kite Unchecked Growth away from the water and the group

**Ancient Protectors**
1. Kill order is Life Warden Gola, then Earthshaper Telu, then Dulhu
2. Stay out of Telu's Bramble Patch, and don't waste damage into Briarskin
- `KICK` need kick — "Gola's Revitalizing Waters — it heals the other two for 15% each time" · ability `Revitalizing Waters`
- `TANK` tank — "Dulhu's Noxious Eruption knocks back" · ability `Noxious Eruption`

**Xeri'tac** — optional
1. Kill or CC the Venom-Crazed Pale Ones before she Consumes them to heal

**Archmage Sol**
1. Three phases — Fire, Frost, Arcane — and the order is only half predictable
2. Personal defensives on every Cinderbolt Storm; Fire is the dangerous one
- `KICK` need kick — "Parasitic Growth, which is how you control the phase order" · ability `Parasitic Growth`

**Yalnu**
1. Stomp the flowers from Genesis immediately or they become Swift Sproutlings
2. Dodge the Colossal Blow cone — it stuns

## Grimrail Depot

Rocketspark and Borka → Nitrogg Thundertower → Skylord Tovra.

**Rocketspark and Borka**
1. Melee take Borka, ranged take Rocketspark
2. Move out of the small red rocket circles
3. When Rocketspark takes off, the tank steers Borka's Mad Dash into him to bring him down
- `CD` healer — "Borka's Slam interrupts casting: plan around it" · ability `Slam`

**Nitrogg Thundertower**
1. At 60% he mans the Assault Cannon: take Blackrock Munitions off the soldiers and destroy it

**Skylord Tovra**
1. Rakun electrifies parts of the cart as it flies over — move off them immediately

## Shadowmoon Burial Grounds

Sadana Bloodfury → Nhallish → Bonemaw → Ner'zhul.

**Sadana Bloodfury**
1. Never be caught by Daggerfall
2. Dark Eclipse: stand in a Lunar Purity rune, it takes 80% off

**Nhallish**
1. Kite away from the Planar Shift vortex
2. Soul Steal: kill your own soul quickly for the buff and to cut Soul Shred

**Bonemaw**
1. Inhale pulls you in — stand in a Corpse Breath puddle to hold your ground
2. Dodge the Body Slam frontal

**Ner'zhul**
1. Omen of Death does proximity damage and baits ranged — run it out
2. Ritual of Bones: pre-assign who cleaves which skeletons

## Skyreach

Ranjit → Araknath → Rukhran → High Sage Viryx. *(Two sources agree.)*

**Ranjit**
1. Dodge Piercing Rush — it charges a random player
2. Four Winds splits the platform into rotating sections: move with them

**Araknath**
1. Block the healing beams from the room's constructs or he heals through everything
2. Burst is unavoidable and stacks — push damage early
- `TANK` tank — "Smash: step aside during the cast" · ability `Smash`

**Rukhran**
1. Summon Solar Flare fixates: slow it, stun it, kill it — and away from the ash piles
- `TANK` tank — "stay in melee or Screech hits everyone; Pierce Armor stacks" · ability `Pierce Armor`

**High Sage Viryx**
1. Cast Down drops Solar Zealots that carry players to the edge — stun, slow or root them first
2. Kill the Shield Constructs quickly, they block half his damage
- (no role) — "Move out of the Lens Flare beam, it leaves fire" · ability `Lens Flare`

## What changed in the re-source

The first pass had **no mechanics at all** for Soulbinder Nyami, Slave Watcher
Crushto, Xeri'tac or Ancient Protectors, and one search summary had garbled
Nyami's name to "Soulbinder Kaathar". All four are now sourced, and the name
is confirmed as Soulbinder Nyami.

Remaining uncertainty is one ordering question (Magmolatus vs Slave Watcher
Crushto first) rather than missing content.
