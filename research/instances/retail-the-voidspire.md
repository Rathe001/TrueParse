# The Voidspire — retail (Midnight, launch tier)

- **Journal name:** The Voidspire
- **Type:** raid, 6 bosses
- **Sources:** MythicTrap *Voidspire* per-boss guides and Method *The Voidspire*
  per-boss Heroic guides, both read 2026-09-08. Reconciled.
- **Status:** drafted

Encounter order: Imperator Averzian → Vorasius → Fallen-King Salhadaar →
Vaelgor and Ezzorak → Lightblinded Vanguard → Crown of the Cosmos.

---

## 1. Imperator Averzian

Single phase. The arena is a 3x3 tile board; the boss claims tiles through
Voidshapers and three in a row wipes the raid.

**core**
1. Three claimed tiles in a row is a wipe — stop the Voidshapers finishing Void Rupture
2. Put the Umbral Collapse soaks on the Voidshapers: that is what strips their 99% immunity
3. Keep the boss off claimed tiles or it deals 75% more and takes 99% less

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "Pitch Bulwark from the Stalwarts and Annihilators, or they shield up" · ability `Pitch Bulwark` · *Method*
- `TANK` tank — "Blackening Wounds takes 4% of your health a stack: swap around 8, and the adds fixate whoever is highest" · ability `Blackening Wounds` · *both*
- `CD` healer — "Dark Upheaval, all fight" · ability `Dark Upheaval` · *both*
- (no role) — "Kill the Voidshapers before they cap energy or they become Obsidian Endwalkers" · ability `Shadow's Advance` · *Method*

Only two of the three Voidshapers can be stopped per cycle — the raid picks
which two.

## 2. Vorasius

**core**
1. Somebody is always in melee, or Overpowering Pulse wipes the raid
2. The tank soaks every Shadowclaw Slam circle
3. Break two Crystal Walls with Blisterburst explosions before Void Breath sweeps

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "Soak the Slam. The first two leave a long physical vulnerability, so swap after them" · ability `Shadowclaw Slam` · *both*
- `CD` healer — "Primordial Roar, and every Blisterburst explosion" · ability `Primordial Roar` · *both*
- (no role) — "Kite the fixated Blistercreeps to a wall and kill them on it" · ability `Blisterburst` · *both*
- (no role) — "Run opposite the casting hand for Void Breath" · ability `Void Breath` · *Method*

## 3. Fallen-King Salhadaar

Single phase; the 100-energy Entropic Unraveling is the burn window.

**core**
1. Kill the Concentrated Void orbs before they reach the boss — one arriving is a wipe
2. Interrupt or CC every Fractured Projection before Shadow Fracture lands
3. Entropic Unraveling: the boss takes 25% more — dodge the rotating beams and dump

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "the Fractured Projections — any CC works too" · ability `Shadow Fracture` · *both*
- `TANK` tank — "Destabilizing Strikes stacks: swap. Keep the boss kited away from the orbs" · ability `Destabilizing Strikes` · *both*
- `CD` healer — "Dark Radiation after each orb dies, and the Twisting Obscurity DoT" · ability `Dark Radiation` · *both*
- (no role) — "Drop Despotic Command at the edges: the puddles are permanent and leave a healing absorb" · ability `Despotic Command` · *both*

## 4. Vaelgor and Ezzorak

Two dragons, one intermission at 100 energy.

**core**
1. Twilight Bond: keep them 15 yards apart and within 10% health, or both hit twice as hard
2. Void Howl: stack up so the Voidorbs spawn together, then grip and burst them
3. Nullzone: run out to snap your tether — the tank snaps last

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "Voidbolt from the Voidorbs" · ability `Voidbolt` · *both*
- `TANK` tank — "Swap on Rakfang and Vaelwing; dip into Nullbeam for the stacks first" · ability `Rakfang` · *both*
- `CD` healer — "the Midnight Manifestation stacks, and the Midnight Flames intermission" · ability `Midnight Manifestation` · *both*
- (no role) — "Never stand behind them — Tail Lash knocks back and bleeds" · ability `Tail Lash` · *Method*
- (no role) — "Rotate the Gloom soakers: the debuff stops you soaking twice" · ability `Gloom` · *both*

Dread Breath is a dispellable fear on a random player — see SCHEMA GAPS.

## 5. Lightblinded Vanguard

Paladin-themed council of three. Method names them Commander Venel, General
Bellamy and Chaplain Senn; MythicTrap names the third "Lightblood" — see
CONFLICTS.

**core**
1. Kill all three evenly: when one dies, Retribution ramps the survivors every 2 seconds
2. Each 100-energy aura: get out of range, especially Senn's pacify
3. Execution Sentence: spread the soaks, one each per set

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "Blinding Light, once the Sacred Shield is broken" · ability `Blinding Light` · *both*
- `PURGE` need purge — "Mass Dispel the Divine Shields early" · ability `Divine Shield` · *both*
- `TANK` tank — "Swap after Judgment: it multiplies the hit that follows it" · ability `Judgment` · *both*
- `CD` healer — "Light Infused climbs 25% with every ultimate, on top of Sacred Toll and Searing Radiance" · ability `Light Infused` · *both*
- (no role) — "Dodge the Divine Toll shield waves, they silence" · ability `Divine Toll` · *both*

## 6. Crown of the Cosmos

Three phases, two intermissions.

**core**
1. Shoot the Silverstrike Arrows through the Sentinels to strip Umbral Tether — they cannot die with it up
2. Aspect of the End: break the tethers in order, ranged first, then melee, tank last
3. Ride the Dark Rush feathers to the next slice before Devouring Cosmos covers yours

**notes**
- `LUST` need lust — "phase 3" · ability `Devouring Cosmos` · *Method*
- `KICK` need kick — "Void Barrage on the Voidspawns, before they cap energy and go immune" · ability `Void Barrage` · *Method*
- `TANK` tank — "Rift Slash drops all your stats 10% a stack: swap at two or three" · ability `Rift Slash` · *both*
- `CD` healer — "Null Corona: heal it off rather than dispel it, the absorb jumps with whatever is left" · ability `Null Corona` · *both*
- (no role) — "Hold against the Stellar Emission pull through the intermissions" · ability `Stellar Emission` · *both*

## SCHEMA GAPS this raid exposes

Two capability words the notes system does not have, both needed here:

1. **Fear removal.** Vaelgor and Ezzorak's Dread Breath is a dispellable fear.
   `KN` capability keys are magic / curse / poison / disease / bleed / purge /
   soothe / kick / stun / lust — there is no fear entry, and a fear is not
   reliably a magic dispel across specs (Tremor Totem, Fear Ward, Berserker
   Rage and a magic dispel all answer it differently).
2. **Mass Dispel specifically.** Lightblinded Vanguard's Divine Shield is
   removed by Mass Dispel, not by an ordinary purge. Tagging it `PURGE` would
   show the line to every purge-capable spec, most of which cannot do it.

Both are worth resolving before this raid ships, since in each case the wrong
tag tells a player they have a job they cannot do.
