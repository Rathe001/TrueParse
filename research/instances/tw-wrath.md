# Timewalking pool — Wrath of the Lich King

- **Type:** 6 legacy dungeons, **thin treatment**: bosses and the two or three
  lines that still kill people. No trash.
- **Sources:** Icy Veins *WotLK Classic* per-dungeon guides, read 2026-09-08.
  Single source, but a complete and consistent set.
- **Status:** drafted

---

## Utgarde Keep

Prince Keleseth → Dalronn and Skarvald → Ingvar the Plunderer.

**Prince Keleseth**
1. Frost Tomb traps a player — target the block and break it out
2. Kill the Vrykul Skeletons as they keep coming

**Dalronn and Skarvald**
1. Kill them together, or the survivor comes back as a ghost
2. Skarvald's Charge stuns and hits hard

**Ingvar the Plunderer**
1. Face him away — Cleave and Smash are frontals
2. Phase 2: line-of-sight the Shadow Axe behind a pillar

## The Nexus

Commander Stoutbeard (Horde) / Commander Kolurg (Alliance) →
Grand Magus Telestra → Anomalus → Ormorok the Tree-Shaper → Keristrasza.

**Commander Stoutbeard / Kolurg**
1. Spread — Whirlwind hits everything near him
- `FEAR` need fear — "Frightening Shout, or it runs somebody into another pack" · ability `Frightening Shout`

**Grand Magus Telestra**
1. Spread for Firebomb
2. At 50% she splits into Mirror Images — interrupt or stun what they cast

**Anomalus**
1. He is immune while a Chaotic Rift is open — kill the rift first
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Arcane Attraction" · ability `Arcane Attraction`

**Ormorok the Tree-Shaper**
1. Never stand in front of him or directly behind
2. Spell Reflection sends your own casts back — stop casting
3. Crystal Spikes knock you up

**Keristrasza**
1. Never stand still: Intense Cold stacks the moment you stop
2. Break the roots immediately, they are what kills you

## Azjol-Nerub

Krik'thir the Gatewatcher → Hadronox → Anub'arak.

**Krik'thir the Gatewatcher**
1. His melee AoE kills — spread out of it
2. Kill the spider adds quickly

**Hadronox**
1. Kill the adds — they are what heal him
2. Spike ruptures through the burrow phases hit hard
- `POISON` need poison — "Leech Poison — it heals him enormously" · ability `Leech Poison` · *the guide says "dispel this" without naming a school; Leech Poison is a poison, but confirm*

**Anub'arak**
1. The tank faces Pound away from the group
2. Clear your debuffs before he burrows

## Gundrak

Slad'ran → Drakkari Colossus → Moorabi → Eck the Ferocious (Heroic) →
Gal'darah.

**Slad'ran**
1. Move out of Poison Nova

**Drakkari Colossus**
1. Move out of the floor puddles at once
2. He swaps between Colossus and Elemental form — the fight changes with him

**Moorabi**
1. Mojo Frenzy makes each cast faster than the last, so the window shrinks
- `KICK` need kick — "the transform cast — if it lands you are fighting a mammoth" · ability `Transformation`

**Eck the Ferocious** — min `h`
1. Face away from Eck Spit: heavy damage and it drains mana
2. Eck Spring resets threat — the tank re-taunts immediately

**Gal'darah**
1. Impaling Charge stacks a debuff across the whole party
2. Heavy AoE, and bleeds on whoever is in melee

## Halls of Lightning

General Bjarngrim → Volkhan → Ionar → Loken.

**General Bjarngrim**
1. Kill the adds or they heal him
2. His stance decides what he does — read it before committing

**Volkhan**
1. Kill the Molten Golems before they pile up
2. Move for Shattering Stomp

**Ionar**
1. Spread — Static Overload jumps to whoever is close
2. The Sparks chase, and hit harder the longer they live

**Loken**
1. Pulsing Shockwave hurts *more* the further away you are — stay close
2. Lightning Nova is the burst: that is what the healer plans for

---

## The Forge of Souls

Bronjahm → Devourer of Souls.

**Bronjahm**
1. Kill the Corrupt Soul fragments before they reach him and heal him
2. At 35% Soulstorm starts — stand on top of him, that is the safe spot
- `FEAR` need fear — "the fear during Soulstorm drags people out into it" · ability `Soulstorm`

**Devourer of Souls**
1. Stop attacking during Mirrored Soul — your damage comes back at the marked player
2. Stay behind him for the Wailing Souls beam
- `KICK` need kick — "Phantom Blast, every cast" · ability `Phantom Blast`

## Note

A third and fourth `FEAR` case here (The Nexus' Frightening Shout, Bronjahm's
Soulstorm fear), both with the same signature as the Cataclysm ones: the fear
is not incidental damage, it is the thing that moves you into the mechanic
that kills you.
