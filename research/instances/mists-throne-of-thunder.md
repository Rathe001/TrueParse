# Throne of Thunder — MoP Classic

- **Journal name:** Throne of Thunder
- **Type:** raid, 13 bosses (Ra-den is Heroic-only). **Boss-only** — no trash.
- **Sources:** Skycoach *Throne of Thunder Guide* and Overgear *Throne of
  Thunder Overview* (both MoP Classic, read 2026-09-08). Reconciled.
  Icy Veins has **no** MoP Classic guide for this tier — its
  `throne-of-thunder-raiding-guides` path 404s, so the dungeon files' Icy
  Veins caveat does not apply here; these are two genuinely independent
  sources.
- **Status:** drafted

Boss order: Jin'rokh the Breaker → Horridon → Council of Elders → Tortos →
Megaera → Ji-Kun → Durumu the Forgotten → Primordius → Dark Animus →
Iron Qon → Twin Consorts → Lei Shen → Ra-den.

---

## 1. Jin'rokh the Breaker

**core**
1. Stack in the newest Conductive Water for the damage and healing buff
2. Focused Lightning: run the orb away and detonate it outside the pool
3. Get out of the water and stack up when Lightning Storm starts

**notes**
- `LUST` need lust — "about 20 seconds after the first Conductive Water" · *Skycoach*
- `TANK` tank — "Static Wound stacks: swap, and reposition after Thundering Throw" · ability `Static Wound`
- `CD` healer — "Lightning Storm" · ability `Lightning Storm`

Seven-minute enrage. No interrupts or dispels in this fight.

---

## 2. Horridon

**core**
1. Every door brings a different troll type — clear the adds before the next one opens
2. Never stand in front of him or behind him: Double Swipe and Charge
3. Phase five: kill War-God Jalak before Bestial Cry stacks up

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "the Zandalari Dinomancer's Dino-Mending, and the door casters" · ability `Dino-Mending`
- `TANK` tank — "Triple Puncture: swap on stacks" · ability `Triple Puncture`
- `CD` healer — "each door transition" · ability `Bestial Cry`

Twelve-minute enrage.

---

## 3. Council of Elders

Four trolls: Frost King Malakk, Kazra'jin, Sul the Sandcrawler, High
Priestess Mar'li. One is empowered at a time.

**core**
1. All four have to fall together — push whichever one is empowered
2. Stack to split Frostbite
3. Stop damaging Kazra'jin during Overload: it reflects

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "Sand Bolt and Wrath of the Loa, every chance" · ability `Sand Bolt`
- `POISON` need poison — "Blazing Sunlight" · ability `Blazing Sunlight` · *Skycoach*
- `DISEASE` need disease — "Deadly Plague" · ability `Deadly Plague` · *Skycoach*
- `TANK` tank — "Swap on Frigid Assault; pick up Malakk and Sul" · ability `Frigid Assault`

No enrage.

---

## 4. Tortos

**core**
1. Kick a Whirl Turtle into Tortos to interrupt Furious Stone Breath — one has to be ready for every cast
2. Keep moving out of the Rockfall circles
3. Kill the Vampiric Cave Bats quickly, they drain the raid

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "One holds Tortos, the other takes the bats" · ability `Snapping Bite`
- `CD` healer — "Quake Stomp" · ability `Quake Stomp`

Eight-minute enrage. The turtle kick is the interrupt — no spell interrupt works.

---

## 5. Megaera

**core**
1. Pick one head kill order and stick to it
2. Cinders: run it over the icy ground before it comes off
3. Rampage: every cooldown, every time

**notes**
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Cinders — but the target crosses the ice first" · ability `Cinders` · *both are explicit that the ice comes before the dispel*
- `TANK` tank — "Pick up the remaining heads and face the breaths away" · ability `Rampage`
- `CD` healer — "every Rampage" · ability `Rampage`

---

## 6. Ji-Kun

**core**
1. Take a feather to your assigned nest and kill the Hatchlings before they grow
2. Catch the Feed Young globules for the buff and keep them off the platform
3. Stack and heal through Quills; hold the centre through Down Draft

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "Swap on Talon Rake and Infected Talons" · ability `Talon Rake`
- `CD` healer — "Quills" · ability `Quills`

---

## 7. Durumu the Forgotten

**core**
1. Colour phase: stand in the beams to reveal the adds — kill the Crimson Fogs, avoid the Azure
2. Disintegration Beam: follow the safe path and never stop moving
3. Ten-minute enrage; Obliterate wipes the raid

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "Swap on Hard Stare" · ability `Hard Stare`
- `CD` healer — "the Disintegration phase" · ability `Disintegration Beam`

Overgear calls this "one of the hardest fights in this raid".

---

## 8. Primordius

**core**
1. Take Mutagenic Pools until you are fully mutated, then go to the boss
2. Unmutated players kill the Living Fluids to make more pools
3. Once fully mutated, stay out of further pools or you pick up harmful mutations

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "Malformed Blood: swap. Stay out of the Primordial Strike cone" · ability `Malformed Blood`
- `CD` healer — "Volatile Pathogen" · ability `Volatile Pathogen`

Eight-minute enrage.

---

## 9. Dark Animus

**core**
1. Never let Dark Animus reach 100 Anima — Full Power is a wipe
2. Only activate the golems you mean to, and kill the small ones apart from each other
3. Stand in the Anima Ring impact zones so it cannot spread

**notes**
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Matter Swap, after a short delay" · ability `Matter Swap`
- `TANK` tank — "Split the Anima and Massive Anima Golems between you" · ability `Siphon Anima`
- `CD` healer — "the Interrupting Jolt silences" · ability `Interrupting Jolt`

---

## 10. Iron Qon

**core**
1. Rotate stacking groups for Unleashed Flame, then spread
2. Wind phase: break the Arcing Lightning stuns and stay out of the tornadoes
3. Frost phase: keep off the shielded side of the Dead Zone and free frozen players

**notes**
- `LUST` need lust — "the final phase" · ability `Rising Anger`
- `TANK` tank — "Swap the debuff stacks; find tornado-free ground in the last phase" · ability `Fist Smash`
- `CD` healer — "the final phase, where everything overlaps" · ability `Fist Smash`

Twelve-minute enrage.

---

## 11. Twin Consorts

*Name conflict: Warcraft Tavern's boss list calls this encounter "Twin
Empyreans"; both mechanics sources call it "Twin Consorts", which is also the
name the addon would key on. Confirm against the journal.*

Lu'lin (night) and Suen (day).

**core**
1. Night: spread for Cosmic Barrage, avoid the sleep clouds, kite Beast of Nightmares
2. Day: stand in an Ice Comet's shade to drop Blazing Radiance stacks
3. Keep the Comets alive — later phases need them

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "Alternate on Suen for Fan of Flames; take Beast of Nightmares" · ability `Fan of Flames`
- `CD` healer — "the Darkness phase, and the Corrupted Healing debuffs" · ability `Cosmic Barrage`

---

## 12. Lei Shen

**core**
1. Phase 1: split to the four quadrants and stop the conduits levelling up
2. Transitions: go to your assigned corner and stay out of the overloaded quadrant
3. Phase 2: face away for Fusion Slash, dodge the Lightning Whip lines, kill the Ball Lightning

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "Swap on the stacks and mind which conduit you are next to" · ability `Decapitate`
- `CD` healer — "both transitions, and Violent Gale Winds in phase 3" · ability `Violent Gale Winds`
- (no role) — "Stack for Static Shock and soak the Bouncing Bolt impacts" · ability `Static Shock`

Ten-minute enrage. **Lust is a strategy choice** — Skycoach offers early phase 1
or the start of phase 3. Not written as a single call.

---

## 13. Ra-den — Heroic only

Everything here carries `min = "h"`.

**core**
1. Phase 1: manage the Vita and Anima exchanges and kill the Materials balls in sequence
2. Pass Unstable Vita to the furthest player; stack for the Unstable Anima dispels
3. From 40% it is a burn: Ruin scales with what you consumed

**notes**
- `LUST` need lust — "40%, when Ruin starts" · ability `Ruin`
- `TANK` tank — "Rotate cooldowns for Fatal Strike; stay close for the Vita exchanges" · ability `Fatal Strike`
- `CD` healer — "the Ruin phase from 40%" · ability `Ruin`

Ten-minute enrage.
