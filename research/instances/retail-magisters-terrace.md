# Magisters' Terrace — retail (Midnight)

- **Journal name:** Magisters' Terrace (Icy Veins spells it this way; Method's
  page says "Magister's Terrace". The TBC original is Magisters' Terrace —
  confirm against the journal before registering.)
- **Type:** dungeon, 4 bosses
- **Pool:** not in the Season 2 keystone pool
- **Sources:** Icy Veins *Magisters' Terrace Dungeon Guide* and Method
  *Magisters' Terrace Mythic+*, both read 2026-09-08. Reconciled.
- **Status:** drafted

Boss order: Arcanotron → Seranel Sunlash → Gemellus → Degentrius.

---

## Arcanotron

*Name conflict: Icy Veins "Arcanotron", Method "Arcanotron Custos".*

**core**
1. Soak the Energy Orbs before the boss eats them, and watch your Unstable Energy stacks
2. Refueling Protocol: it takes 20% more damage — burn it
3. Stay out of the Arcane Residue

**notes**
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Ethereal Shackles, it lands on two" · ability `Ethereal Shackles` · *both*
- `TANK` tank — "Repulsing Slam: defensive, and mind the knockback" · ability `Repulsing Slam` · *both*
- `CD` healer — "Arcane Expulsion, and the Unstable Energy stacks through Refueling Protocol" · ability `Arcane Expulsion` · *both*

---

## Seranel Sunlash

**core**
1. Runic Mark: clear it in the Suppression Zone, spread out so Null Reaction doesn't stack
2. Be inside the Suppression Zone before Wave of Silence finishes or you are pacified for 8s
3. Dodge the arcane barrages

**notes**
- `PURGE` need purge — "Hastening Ward off the boss" · ability `Hastening Ward` · *both*
- `TANK` tank — "Defensive for Hastening Ward if nobody purges it" · ability `Hastening Ward` · *both*

---

## Gemellus

**core**
1. Triplicate copies share one health pool: cleave all of them
2. Neural Link: run to the copy you are linked to and strip its shield
3. Astral Grasp pulls you in — burn a movement ability to get clear of Cosmic Radiation

**notes**
- `CD` healer — "Astral Grasp, for anyone with no movement cooldown left" · ability `Cosmic Radiation` · *IV*
- `TANK` tank — "Under 50% it targets you like a DPS" · ability `Triplicate` · *Method*
- (no role) — "Stay out of the Void Secretions" · *both*

---

## Degentrius

**core**
1. Never touch the Void Torrent beams
2. Split across the arena so both Unstable Void Essence soaks get caught
3. Bounce the Unstable Energy ball — a dropped catch stacks Void Destruction, and stacks wipe

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "Hulking Fragment: step out so it doesn't clip the group, and drop the Stygian Ichor away" · ability `Hulking Fragment` · *both*
- `MAGIC` need magic — "the tank's DoT after Hulking Fragment" · ability `Hulking Fragment` · *Method*
- `CD` healer — "Devouring Entropy, especially over the soaks" · ability `Devouring Entropy` · *both*

## CONFLICTS — not written

- **Runic Mark.** Method lists it as a magic dispel; Icy Veins says it is
  cleared by standing in the Suppression Zone (Feedback). Contradictory, so no
  dispel line was written — the Suppression Zone line covers the action either
  way.
- **Boss 1's name.** "Arcanotron" (IV) vs "Arcanotron Custos" (Method).
- **Degentrius' first mechanic.** Icy Veins describes an Unstable Energy ball
  passed between players; Method describes only the Unstable Void Essence
  soaks. Both lines are written because they are not mutually exclusive, but
  the ball needs confirming.
