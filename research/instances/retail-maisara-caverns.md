# Maisara Caverns — retail (Midnight)

- **Journal name:** Maisara Caverns
- **Type:** dungeon, 3 bosses
- **Pool:** not in the Season 2 keystone pool
- **Sources:** Icy Veins *Maisara Caverns* dungeon guide and Method *Maisara
  Caverns Mythic+* guide, both read 2026-09-08. Reconciled.
- **Status:** drafted

Boss order: Muro'jin & Nekraxx → Vordaza → Rak'tul, Vessel of Souls.

This dungeon has real non-combat gates — the first `TASK` material outside
Season 2.

---

## Muro'jin & Nekraxx

**core**
1. Kill them together: whichever dies first revives or enrages the other
2. Carrion Swoop: the target stands in a Freezing Trap — everyone else stays out of them
3. Don't stand in the Fetid Quillstorm circles

**notes**
- `DISEASE` need disease — "Infected Pinions" · ability `Infected Pinions` · *both*
- `TANK` tank — "Flanking Spear: defensive, or cleanse the bleed" · ability `Flanking Spear` · *both; the bleed cleanse is Method's*
- `CD` healer — "Infected Pinions" · ability `Infected Pinions` · *IV*
- (no role) — "Stand still if Barrage picks you, or it sprays the group" · ability `Barrage` · *Method*

---

## Vordaza

**core**
1. Kite the phantoms into each other — that is the only way they die
2. Dodge the Unmake frontal
3. Necrotic Convergence: burn the Deathshroud shield and dodge the Coalesced Death orbs

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "Drain Soul: defensive on every cast" · ability `Drain Soul` · *both*
- `CD` healer — "the Lingering Dread rot, and again through Necrotic Convergence" · ability `Lingering Dread` · *IV*

---

## Rak'tul, Vessel of Souls

**core**
1. Crush Souls picks three: group up so the Soulbind Totems land close enough to cleave
2. Through the Soulrending Roar gauntlet, lock down the Malignant Souls for the Spectral Residue stacks
3. Sidestep the Volatile Essence puddles

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "the Malignant Souls in the gauntlet, every cast" · ability `Malignant Soul` · *both*
- `TANK` tank — "Spiritbreaker: defensive, and take the Spectral Decay puddle away from the group" · ability `Spiritbreaker` · *both*
- `CD` healer — "Deathgorged Vessel" · ability `Deathgorged Vessel` · *both*

## CONFLICTS — not resolved

- **Necrotic Wave (Dread Souleater).** Method says interrupt it; Icy Veins says
  use defensives for it. Written as a kick with the source flagged — worth an
  in-game check, because a kick line that cannot be kicked is worse than no
  line.
- **Phantom naming.** Icy Veins calls them Unstable Phantoms, Method calls them
  Wrest Phantoms. The core line avoids the name until the journal settles it.
- **Lust.** Neither source makes a call. Left out.
