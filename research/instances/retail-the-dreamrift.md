## Chimaerus, the Undreamt God

Phase 1 loops into an intermission and back. The boss is at 99% damage
reduction (Rift Shroud) through the intermission, so the whole fight is about
add control.

**core**
1. Not one add reaches the boss — Insatiable heals it for 200% of its missing health and doubles its damage
2. Alndust Upheaval: alternate two soak groups; soaking grants Alnsight, which is what lets you damage the adds
3. Consume at 100 energy eats whatever adds are still up and starts the intermission

Vulnerability so they cannot soak twice; MythicTrap describes the same

and the buff names are Method's.
