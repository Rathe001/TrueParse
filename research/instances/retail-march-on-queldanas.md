## 1. Belo'ren, Child of Al'ar

Alternates Phoenix Form and a 30-second Egg Form. Every player is assigned a
**Light Feather** or a **Void Feather** at the pull, and the whole fight is
colour matching.

**core**
1. You are Light or Void: only ever take the mechanic that matches your own feather
2. Only a matching-colour player can interrupt Light Eruption or Void Eruption
3. Egg form: hard swap and burn it before it rebirths — you get 15 seconds

**notes**
- `LUST` need lust — "the egg burn, around 30%" · ability `Death Drop` · *Method*
- `KICK` need kick — "Light and Void Eruption, but only if your feather matches the colour" · ability `Light Eruption` · *Method*
- `TANK` tank — "Guardian's Edict: intercept the cone in your own colour, or the boss gains a stacking 30%" · ability `Guardian's Edict` · *Method*
- `CD` healer — "Burning Heart through every egg phase — and Ashen Benediction cuts your healing 10% a stack" · ability `Ashen Benediction` · *Method*
- (no role) — "Kill the Embers before they cast Rebirth" · ability `Rebirth` · *Method*
- (no role) — "Infused Quills: the matching colour intercepts the beam" · ability `Infused Quills` · *Method*

Each egg phase permanently stacks the healing reduction and doubles the Ember
spawn rate, so the fight gets harder every cycle rather than easier.

## 2. Midnight Falls (L'ura)

Three phases with an intermission after phase 1. The whole encounter is built
on Dawn Crystals: they are made in phase 1 and spent in phase 3, so a raid
that mishandles them cannot finish.

**core**
1. Death's Dirge: the five marked players take the laser in the order the symbols appeared — a miss is a wipe
2. Heal the Dusk Crystals into Dawn Crystals; phase 3 cannot be survived without them
3. The Dark Archangel: stand in the Dawnlight Barrier from a consumed Dawn Crystal

**notes**
- `LUST` need lust — "on the pull" · *Method*
- `KICK` need kick — "the Safeguard Matrices, or they refresh the boss's shield" · ability `Safeguard Prism` · *Method*
- `TANK` tank — "Heaven's Lance: swap after every fifth stack, Impale makes the next one 50% worse" · ability `Heaven's Lance` · *Method*
- `CD` healer — "Shattered Sky, and the Thunderous Well every 2 seconds through phase 3" · ability `Shattered Sky` · *Method*
- (no role) — "Light Siphon: both soaks filled inside 22 seconds or Stellar Implosion wipes" · ability `Light Siphon` · *Method*
- (no role) — "Phase 3: stay inside a Torchbearer's 12-yard circle" · ability `Torchbearer` · *Method*
- (no role) — "Never drop a Dawn Crystal for more than 3 seconds" · ability `Dawn Crystal` · *Method*
- (no role) — "Dark Meltdown: every Void Core dies or Decay wipes the raid" · ability `Dark Meltdown` · *Method*
