# Nexus-Point Xenas — retail (Midnight)

- **Journal name:** Nexus-Point Xenas (Icy Veins spells the hyphen; Method
  writes "Nexus Point Xenas". Confirm against the journal.)
- **Type:** dungeon, 3 bosses
- **Pool:** not in the Season 2 keystone pool
- **Sources:** Icy Veins *Nexus-Point Xenas Dungeon Guide* and Method
  *Nexus Point Xenas M+*, both read 2026-09-08. Reconciled.
- **Status:** drafted

Boss order: Chief Corewright Kasreth → Corewarden Nysarra → Lothraxion.

Three wings (Ethereal, Void, Light) with their own trash identities, and the
richest `TASK` material found so far — including a profession-gated one.

---

## Chief Corewright Kasreth

**core**
1. Never walk through an active Leyline Array
2. Reflux Charge: the target runs into the beams to break the Arrays
3. Outrange Corespark Detonation and stay out of the Arcane Spill it leaves

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "Arcane Zap" · ability `Arcane Zap` · *IV; see CONFLICTS*
- `CD` healer — "Sparkburn, after every Corespark Detonation" · ability `Sparkburn` · *both*
- `TANK` tank — "Drop Flux Collapse and the detonation puddles at the edges" · ability `Flux Collapse` · *Method*

---

## Corewarden Nysarra

**core**
1. Null Vanguard: the Dreadflail and the Grand Nullifier die before Lightscar Flare ends, or Devour the Unworthy goes off
2. Lightscar Flare: the boss takes 300% more damage — everything goes in here
3. Don't cleave the group with Eclipsing Step

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "Nullify from the Grand Nullifiers, every cast" · ability `Nullify` · *Method*
- `TANK` tank — "Umbral Lash and the Void Gash behind it: defensive" · ability `Umbral Lash` · *both*
- `CD` healer — "through Lightscar Flare" · ability `Lightscar Flare` · *both*

---

## Lothraxion

**core**
1. Divine Guile: the real one has the horns of light — hit that one
2. Brilliant Dispersion leaves Fractured Images: stay 5 yards clear of them
3. Stay out of the Flicker dash paths

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "Holy Bolt from the adds — but never kick an image, it causes Core Exposure" · ability `Holy Bolt` · *both; the warning is Method's*
- `TANK` tank — "Searing Rend: defensive, and drop the puddle away from the group" · ability `Searing Rend` · *both*
- `CD` healer — "every Brilliant Dispersion" · ability `Brilliant Dispersion` · *both*

## CONFLICTS — not resolved

- **Arcane Zap.** Icy Veins calls it an interruptible cast; Method says the
  tank uses defensives through it and notes the boss has no melee swing.
  Written as a kick with the source flagged — verify before shipping, since a
  kick line on an uninterruptible cast is worse than no line.
- **Flarebat (Light wing).** Method says purge its Holy Echo buff; Icy Veins
  says outrange a Holy Echo explosion. Contradictory — no line written.
- **Lingering Image.** Method says face away from it; Icy Veins puts Searing
  Rend on it for the tank. Contradictory — no line written.
