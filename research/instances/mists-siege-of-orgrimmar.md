# Siege of Orgrimmar — MoP Classic

- **Journal name:** Siege of Orgrimmar
- **Type:** raid, 14 bosses. **Boss-only** — no trash.
- **Sources:** wow.gg *Siege of Orgrimmar Raid Guide* and AccountShark
  *MoP Classic Siege of Orgrimmar Raid Guide*, both read 2026-09-08.
  Reconciled. Icy Veins has no MoP Classic guide for this tier.
- **Status:** drafted

Boss names match the crawled percentile data in `Data/Percentiles_Mists.lua`
exactly, which is a useful independent check on the journal spelling.

Wings: Vale of Eternal Sorrows (1-4) → Gates of Retribution (5-8) →
The Underhold (9-11) → Downfall (12-14).

---

## 1. Immerseus

**core**
1. Split phase: DPS kill the Sha Puddles, healers heal the Contaminated Puddles to full
2. Grip, stun, root and knock the puddles so none reach the middle
3. Dodge the Swirl sweeps and the geysers

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "Corrosive Blast: face it away and swap on EVERY cast — it stacks 300% damage taken for 45 seconds" · ability `Corrosive Blast`
- (no role) — "Spread so the Sha Bolt pools do not overlap" · ability `Sha Bolt`

No hard enrage — the corruption bar is the clock.

---

## 2. The Fallen Protectors

Rook Stonetoe, He Softfoot, Sun Tenderheart on one shared health pool.

**core**
1. All three have to reach the threshold together
2. Pass the Mark of Anguish along a chain, never sit on it
3. Gather inside Sun's Dark Meditation

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "Sha Sear" · ability `Sha Sear`
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Shadow Word: Bane, fast" · ability `Shadow Word: Bane`
- `TANK` tank — "Sundering Blow stacks: swap. Take the Embodied adds at 66 and 33" · ability `Sundering Blow`
- `CD` healer — "the Vengeful Strikes stun" · ability `Vengeful Strikes`

---

## 3. Norushen

**core**
1. Everyone starts Corrupted: take the orb, clear your role's trial, come back purified
2. Don't send too many DPS in at once — adds spawn in the room when they finish
3. Dodge the expanding Blind Hatred zones

**notes**
- `LUST` need lust — "30%, when Norushen dies and his Final Gift clears everyone's corruption" · ability `Final Gift`
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Lingering Corruption, in the healer's trial" · ability `Lingering Corruption`
- `TANK` tank — "Self Doubt stacks: swap. Your trial is Test of Confidence" · ability `Self Doubt`

---

## 4. Sha of Pride

**core**
1. Keep your Pride low — at 100 it mind-controls you
2. Stack up in Gift of the Titans for the buff
3. Split the Bursting Pride explosions and free anyone in a Corrupted Prison

**notes**
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Mark of Arrogance — but only while you are under Gift of the Titans" · ability `Mark of Arrogance`
- `KICK` need kick — "Mocking Blast from the adds" · ability `Mocking Blast`
- `TANK` tank — "Wounded Pride: swap for its whole 15s duration — it feeds you 5 Pride per melee hit. Never leave melee" · ability `Wounded Pride`

---

## 5. Galakras

**core**
1. Send a team up the towers to kill the commanders while the raid holds the ground waves
2. Line the raid up so the Flames of Galakrond beam is split
3. Kill the Flagbearers

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "the Tidal Shamans' Healing Tide Totem, and Dagryn's Muzzle Spray" · ability `Healing Tide Totem`
- `TANK` tank — "Swap on the stacking fire DoT" · ability `Flames of Galakrond`
- `CD` healer — "Pulsing Flames as it stacks — that is the soft enrage" · ability `Pulsing Flames`
- (no role) — "Fireball soak: melee behind him, ranged 30 yards behind melee" · ability `Flames of Galakrond`

---

## 6. Iron Juggernaut

**core**
1. Assault mode: dodge the fissures and the Mortar Blasts
2. Siege mode: get your back to a wall before Shock Pulse
3. Never cross the Cutter Laser through the Explosive Tar

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "Flame Vents: taunt at 3 stacks (+10% fire damage taken each). A free tank soaks the Crawler Mines" · ability `Flame Vents`
- `CD` healer — "the mine soaks, and the constant Seismic Activity chip" · ability `Seismic Activity`

---

## 7. Kor'kron Dark Shaman

**core**
1. Kill the wolf mounts first
2. Keep Toxicity down — it amplifies every bit of nature damage after it
3. Position them so the Ashen Wall does not cut the room in half

**notes**
- `LUST` need lust — "25%, the burn phase" · ability `Bloodlust`
- `TANK` tank — "Froststorm Strike stacks: swap. Kite Foul Geyser and spread the slimes" · ability `Froststorm Strike`
- `KICK` need kick — "the shamans' direct casts" · ability `Foul Stream`

## 8. General Nazgrim

**core**
1. Watch his stance: in Defensive Stance nobody attacks but the tanks
2. Never let Ravager go off — that is 100 Rage
3. Kill the War Shamans first, then the Assassins and Arcweavers

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick — "Chain Heal from the War Shamans" · ability `Chain Heal`
- `TANK` tank — "Sundering Blow: swap at 3-4 stacks, and mind how much Rage each stance feeds him" · ability `Sundering Blow`
- `CD` healer — "War Song takes about two thirds of the raid's health" · ability `War Song`
- (no role) — "Never turn your back on an Assassin" · ability `Assassin`

---

## 9. Malkorok

**core**
1. Phase 1: every non-tank soaks an Imploding Energy vortex
2. Breath of Y'Shaarj: know your marked sector before it casts
3. Phase 2: stack tight at his face to split Blood Rage, and run Displaced Energy out

**notes**
- `CD` healer — "Ancient Miasma turns your healing into absorbs: blanket the raid rather than spot-heal" · ability `Ancient Miasma`
- `TANK` tank — "Fatal Strike: swap at 10-15 stacks, and stay in melee" · ability `Fatal Strike`

Tight enrage — this is the raid's hardest DPS check before Garrosh.

---

## 10. Spoils of Pandaria

**core**
1. Split into two sides and open crates at a matching pace — the lever runs on that energy
2. Kill the mantid bombs well away from the group
3. Kill the Pandaren Relic spirits for your role's blessing

**notes**
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Torment, before it chains to the nearest player, and Rage of the Empress" · ability `Torment`
- `KICK` need kick — "Forbidden Magic from the Shao-Tien" · ability `Forbidden Magic`
- `TANK` tank — "Take whatever comes out of the Massive crates" · ability `Massive Crate`

Strict time limit.

---

## 11. Thok the Bloodthirsty

**core**
1. Stand at his flank — the front is a cone and the back is the tail
2. Phase 2: outrun the Fixate, and break line of sight through the gates
3. Kill the Kor'kron Jailer for the cage key, and choose the prisoner order deliberately

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "Fearsome Roar: swap at 4 stacks, and hold him away from the raid" · ability `Fearsome Roar`
- `CD` healer — "Deafening Screech, and the Blood Frenzy burst" · ability `Deafening Screech`

Which prisoner Thok eats changes his breath — acid, freeze or fire.

## 12. Siegecrafter Blackfuse

**core**
1. Rotate players onto the conveyor: at least one weapon dies per volley or he shields
2. Never touch the conveyor's Matter Purification beam — it kills outright
3. Scatter the Shockwave Missile rings by moving inward

**notes**
- `TANK` tank — "Electrostatic Charge: swap at 7 stacks. Drag the Shredders 35 yards off him" · ability `Electrostatic Charge`
- `CD` healer — "the Protective Frenzy spikes" · ability `Protective Frenzy`
- (no role) — "Kite the Crawler Mines, then stun them — after a minute they stop being CC-able" · ability `Crawler Mine`

Three weapons reaching the assembly line means a 90% shield.

## 13. Paragons of the Klaxxi

Nine Paragons, three active at a time.

**core**
1. Every Paragon that dies heals and buffs the survivors with Paragon's Purpose
2. Claim the Paragon powers deliberately — each one suits a role
3. Split Iyyokuk's Insane Calculation beams far apart

**notes**
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Injection" · ability `Injection`
- `TANK` tank — "Intercept the Bloodletting creatures; handle Rik'kal's parasites and the Mutation form" · ability `Bloodletting`
- `CD` healer — "Ingenious spreads your healing — take it if it is offered" · ability `Ingenious`
- (no role) — "Break Korven's amber shells, and watch Kaz'tik's Mesmerize" · ability `Mesmerize`

---

## 14. Garrosh Hellscream

Three phases with a Realm of Y'Shaarj intermission roughly every 2.5 minutes.

**core**
1. Interrupt every Touch of Y'Shaarj — a silence works even on the Empowered version
2. CC the minions into the Iron Star path
3. Intermission: cut through the sha adds and stop his power drain

**notes**
- `LUST` need lust — "phase 3" · ability `Hellscream's Warsong`
- `KICK` need kick — "Touch of Y'Shaarj, without exception" · ability `Touch of Y'Shaarj`
- `TANK` tank — "Gripping Despair stacks: swap" · ability `Gripping Despair`
- `CD` healer — "the minion phases, and every empowerment step" · ability `Desecrate`
- (no role) — "Empowered Whirling Corruption adds get killed one at a time" · ability `Whirling Corruption`

Berserk enrage — the fight is a race once phase 3 opens.

**A source rejected wholesale (2026-09-08):** one search result put Immerseus'
Corrosive Blast on Kor'kron Dark Shaman and Norushen's Self Doubt on the

is why five rows above say "no number found" rather than carrying a number.

## Tank pass, 2026-09-08

Verified against a third source specifically for tank swaps, since this is the
current tier. Confirmed: Immerseus (every Corrosive Blast), Norushen (Self
Doubt), Sha of Pride (Wounded Pride), Thok (Fearsome Roar), Iron Juggernaut
(stacking fire DoT), Malkorok (Fatal Strike). Galakras had no tank line and
now does.

**One attribution to distrust:** that source puts *Sundering Blow* on Malkorok
and has him gaining Rage from it. Rage is General Nazgrim's mechanic, and
Sundering Blow belongs to Nazgrim and the Fallen Protectors. Malkorok's tank
mechanic stays Fatal Strike, as wow.gg has it. Treated as a source error, not
a new line.

## Notes on sourcing

The two sources agree on every encounter's shape. wow.gg is far more detailed
per role; AccountShark corroborates the mechanics and occasionally names a
different ability for the same effect (its "Sha Claws" for Malkorok's tank
swap versus wow.gg's "Fatal Strike"). Where they differ only in naming, the
more specific source's name is used and the other is not treated as a
conflict.

**One real conflict, not written:** AccountShark says Thok's Shriek silences
the raid and needs cooldowns pre-placed; wow.gg describes Deafening Screech as
the raid damage and never mentions a silence. No silence line written.
