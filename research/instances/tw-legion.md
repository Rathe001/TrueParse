# Timewalking pool — Legion

- **Type:** 6 legacy dungeons, **thin treatment**: bosses and the two or three
  lines that still kill people. No trash.
- **Source:** Icy Veins per-dungeon guides, read 2026-09-08. Legion dungeons
  live under `-mythic-guide` rather than `-dungeon-guide` on that site.
  Single source.
- **Status:** drafted

---

## Black Rook Hold

The Amalgam of Souls → Illysanna Ravencrest → Smashspite the Hateful →
Lord Kur'talos Ravencrest.

**The Amalgam of Souls**
1. Dodge the Swirling Scythe frontal
2. Reap Soul is a 180° frontal — the tank points it away
- `CD` healer — "Soul Burst" · ability `Soul Burst`

**Illysanna Ravencrest**
1. Brutal Glaive bounces between players — spread
2. Phase 2: kite the Eye Beams fixate

**Smashspite the Hateful**
1. Hateful Charge stacks — rotate who soaks it
2. Earthshaking Stomp and Fel Vomitus overlap: position for both
- `TANK` tank — "Brutal Haymaker: defensive" · ability `Brutal Haymaker`

**Lord Kur'talos Ravencrest**
1. Dodge the Whirling Blade frontal
2. Phase 2: top the group up before Shadow Bolt Volley
- `TANK` tank — "Unerring Shear: defensive" · ability `Unerring Shear`

Dungeon-wide: `KICK` on Arcane Blitz and Fel Frenzy; `MAGIC` dispel on Soul
Blade.

## Court of Stars

Patrol Captain Gerdo → Talixae Flamewreath → Advisor Melandrus.

**Patrol Captain Gerdo**
1. Dodge the Resonant Slash cone
2. He enrages at 25%
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Arcane Lockdown — or jump three times to shake it" · ability `Arcane Lockdown`
- `TASK` — "Disable the five Signaling Beacons first, or he calls adds"
- `TASK` — "Alchemy skips his enrage phase outright"

**Talixae Flamewreath**
1. Burn the imps from Infernal Eruption the moment they land
2. Stack for the eruption
- `KICK` need kick — "Withering Soul" · ability `Withering Soul`
- `CD` healer — "Burning Intensity" · ability `Burning Intensity`

**Advisor Melandrus**
1. Bait Blade Surge — it charges the furthest player — somewhere safe
2. Keep the boss away from where his images already are, Piercing Gale follows
- `CD` healer — "Slicing Maelstrom on the phase change" · ability `Slicing Maelstrom`

**Objective:** `TASK` — "Find the spy: collect the clues before the last boss."

## Darkheart Thicket

Archdruid Glaidalis → Oakheart → Dresaron → Shade of Xavius.

**Archdruid Glaidalis**
1. Cleave the Nightmare Abominations while staying out of the puddles
2. Grievous Leap stacks a bleed — defensives once it is high

**Oakheart**
1. Strangling Roots: focus the roots to free whoever is caught
2. Pre-heal and use defensives for Shattered Earth

**Dresaron**
1. Be at full health before Earthshaking Roar
2. Down Draft: use a speed boost and avoid the Corrupted Dragon Eggs

**Shade of Xavius**
1. Be at full health before Apocalyptic Nightmare
2. Induced Paranoia: get away from whoever has it, immediately

Dungeon-wide: `KICK` on Dread Inferno, Shadow Bolt and Tormenting Eye.

## Neltharion's Lair

Rokmora → Ularogg Cragshaper → Naraxas → Dargrul the Underking.

**Rokmora**
1. Kill the Blightshard Skitters before they explode
2. Don't stand on Crystalline Ground

**Ularogg Cragshaper**
1. Destroy the Bellowing Idols during the cast, or the ceiling comes down
- `TANK` tank — "Sunder: mitigate it" · ability `Sunder`

**Naraxas**
1. Do not get caught by the Spiked Tongue drag — that is a death
2. Toxic Retch stacks on top of the falling projectiles

**Dargrul the Underking**
1. Get behind a Crystal Spike for Magma Wave
2. Stop the Molten Charskin: its damage escalates every step it takes
- `KICK`/`STUN` — "the Charskin's cast, or stun it" · ability `Magma Sculptor`

## Vault of the Wardens

Tirathon Saltheril → Inquisitor Tormentorum → Ash'Golm → Glazer →
Cordana Felsong.

**Tirathon Saltheril**
1. Never stand in the Hatred beam in Havoc form — it kills outright
2. Vengeance form: stay out from between the Fel Chain glaives
- `KICK` need kick — "Furious Blast after Swoop, or the target dies" · ability `Furious Blast`

**Inquisitor Tormentorum**
1. Flesh to Stone stacks to 10 and petrifies you permanently
- `KICK` need kick — "Frightening Shout from the Mindflayers, and Mind Flay" · ability `Frightening Shout`
- `CURSE` need curse — "Seed of Corruption from the Shadowmoon Warlock — it hits the whole group" · ability `Seed of Corruption`

**Ash'Golm**
1. Pyroclast boulders knock you off the edge
2. Kill the Embers before they reach a lava patch
- `KICK` need kick — "Sear from the Embers" · ability `Sear`

**Glazer**
1. Don't let the Pulse orbs hit you — each one splits into four
2. Focusing phase: Overload from the lens kills the party

**Cordana Felsong**
1. Creeping Doom comes in waves — find the gap
2. Detonating Moonglaive leaves a 50% damage amp
- `TANK` tank — "Knockdown Kick will throw you off the edge" · ability `Knockdown Kick`

## Eye of Azshara

**Open-world layout: the first four can be done in any order**, and Wrath of
Azshara is the last. Worth noting for the tracker — the boss-order assumption
that holds elsewhere does not hold here.

**Warlord Parjesh**
1. Crashing Wave after the rush is the big hit
- `KICK` need kick — "Restoration from the Crestriders" · ability `Restoration`

**Lady Hatecoil**
1. Never be in the water during Static Nova
2. Focused Lightning destroys the safe platforms — mind where you stand
- `KICK` need kick — "Aqua Spout, top priority" · ability `Aqua Spout`
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Polymorph: Fish" · ability `Polymorph: Fish`

**Serpentrix**
1. Toxic Wound puddles stack up fast — keep moving
2. Submerge spawns heads that all need interrupting
- `KICK` need kick — "Blazing Nova and Arcane Blast on both heads" · ability `Blazing Nova`

**King Deepbeard**
1. Break the Gaseous Bubbles shield with damage before it explodes
2. Don't stand in the cracks for Aftershock

**Wrath of Azshara** — final
1. Massive Deluge is a huge frost zone: get out
2. Crushing Depths hits for a percentage of your health
- `KICK` need kick — "Arcane Bomb" · ability `Arcane Bomb`
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Arcane Bomb, to stop it tracking" · ability `Arcane Bomb`

## Note for the implementation

**Eye of Azshara has no fixed boss order.** The tracker derives "which stretch
am I on" from bosses killed, and a free-order dungeon breaks that the same way
raid wings did. Since trash is now keystone-only and this dungeon is
Timewalking-only, the practical answer is probably the same as the raid one:
list the bosses rather than claim which is next.
