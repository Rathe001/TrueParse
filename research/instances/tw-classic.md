# Timewalking pool — Classic

- **Type:** 6 legacy dungeons, **thin treatment**: bosses and the two or three
  lines that still kill people. No trash.
- **Sources:** Icy Veins *WoW Classic* per-dungeon guides, read 2026-09-08.
  Single source. Much of this content genuinely has no mechanics worth a note
  — that is recorded rather than padded.
- **Status:** drafted

**Naming note for the registry.** The Timewalking pool calls these
"Dire Maul - Warpwood Quarter" and "Dire Maul - Capital Gardens", but every
guide calls them Dire Maul East and Dire Maul West. Likewise Stratholme's
"Main Gate" and "Service Entrance" are universally written as Live and Undead.
Whatever the journal actually returns is what the registry must key on —
another argument for instanceID over name.

## The Deadmines

Rhahk'Zor → Miner Johnson (rare) → Sneed's Shredder → Gilnid → Mr. Smite →
Cookie → Captain Greenskin → Edwin VanCleef.

Almost nothing here threatens a max-level group. The three that do:

**Sneed's Shredder**
- `FEAR` need fear — "Terrify" · ability `Terrify`

**Mr. Smite**
1. He changes weapon every 30% health and stuns the group as he does it

**Cookie**
1. He runs at low health and pulls whatever he runs past

**Edwin VanCleef**
1. Two stealthed adds join on the pull — CC them

## Zul'Farrak

Antu'sul → Witch Doctor Zum'rah → Shadowpriest Sezz'ziz and Nekrum Gutchewer
→ Chief Ukorz Sandscalp → Gahz'rilla.

**Antu'sul**
1. His basilisk adds outlive him — deal with them or they follow you
- `KICK` need kick — "his heals, they are long casts" · ability `Healing Wave`

**Witch Doctor Zum'rah**
1. Clear the graves before you pull or he raises zombies out of them
- `KICK` need kick — "his casts are slow and easy to catch" · ability `Shadow Bolt`

**Shadowpriest Sezz'ziz and Nekrum Gutchewer**
1. Clear the pyramid event first — that is 98 trolls
- `FEAR` need fear — "Psychic Scream" · ability `Psychic Scream`
- `KICK` need kick — "their heals" · ability `Heal`

**Chief Ukorz Sandscalp**
1. He cleaves everything in front, and fights alongside Ruuzlu
- `SOOTHE` need soothe — "his enrage" · ability `Enrage`

**Gahz'rilla**
1. His knockback can throw you into packs you have not cleared
2. Needs the Mallet of Zul'Farrak to summon at all

## Dire Maul — Warpwood Quarter (Dire Maul East)

Pusillin → Lethtendris → Hydrospawn → Zevrim Thornhoof →
Alzzin the Wildshaper.

**Pusillin**
1. Four Wildspawn Imps come with him
- `KICK` need kick — "Fire Blast" · ability `Fire Blast`

**Lethtendris**
1. Kill her imp Pimgib first
- `CURSE` need curse — "Curse of Tongues" · ability `Curse of Tongues`

**Hydrospawn**
1. Hydrolings spawn on the pull — kill them or burn the boss through it

**Zevrim Thornhoof**
1. Sacrifice pulls a party member out and puts a heavy DoT on them — stay at max range

**Alzzin the Wildshaper**
1. He changes form and the fight changes with it
2. AoE the imp waves at low health
- `KICK` need kick — "his heals in tree form" · ability `Healing Touch`

## Dire Maul — Capital Gardens (Dire Maul West)

Tendris Warpwood → Magister Kalendris → Tsu'zee (rare) → Illyanna Ravenoak →
Immol'thar → Prince Tortheldrin.

**Tendris Warpwood**
1. Treants join mid-fight if the trash is still up

**Magister Kalendris**
- `MAGIC` need magic — "Shadow Word: Pain" · ability `Shadow Word: Pain`
- `KICK` need kick — "Dominate Mind" · ability `Dominate Mind`

**Immol'thar**
1. Kill the Eyes or their debuff stacks past what the healer can hold
2. His teleport can drop you for fall damage

**Prince Tortheldrin**
1. Arcane Blast resets threat — stop attacking when it goes off

## Stratholme — Main Gate (Live side)

The Unforgiven (optional) → Timmy the Cruel → Postmaster Malown (optional) →
Cannon Master Willey → Archivist Galford →
Grand Crusader Dathrohan / Balnazzar.

**Cannon Master Willey**
1. AoE the adds down, or use the cannons in the room

**Archivist Galford**
1. Burning Winds stuns the tank and hits hard — the healer covers it

**Grand Crusader Dathrohan / Balnazzar**
1. He becomes Balnazzar at 50%
- `FEAR` need fear — "Psychic Scream: ranged and healers stay out of range" · ability `Psychic Scream`
- `KICK`/`STUN` — "Domination — CC works too" · ability `Domination`

**Timmy the Cruel**
- `SOOTHE` need soothe — "his enrage" · ability `Enrage`

## Stratholme — Service Entrance (Undead side)

Magistrate Barthilas → Nerub'enkan → Baroness Anastari → Maleki the Pallid →
Ramstein the Gorger → Baron Rivendare.

**Magistrate Barthilas**
1. Drops the Key to the City — you need it for the gates beyond

**Nerub'enkan**
1. Kill the adds as they are summoned; Encasing Webs pins people

**Baroness Anastari**
1. Possess takes a party member and gives them 2000% health until you bring them to 50%
2. Her Silence can lock the healer out for 10 seconds — have a second healer ready

**Maleki the Pallid**
1. Ice Tomb stuns the tank for 10 seconds and drops threat — be ready for it

**Ramstein the Gorger**
1. Knockout resets threat: re-taunt immediately

**Baron Rivendare**
1. Unholy Aura burns anyone in melee
2. Adds keep coming — kill them before they stack up

## Honest note on this pool

Classic dungeons at Timewalking are mostly threat-free, and this file reflects
that: several bosses get no line at all because they genuinely do not have a
mechanic worth one. That is the thin treatment working as intended, not
missing research. The Deadmines in particular is four short lines for eight
bosses.
