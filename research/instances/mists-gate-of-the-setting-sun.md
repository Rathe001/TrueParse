Boss order: Saboteur Kip'tilak -> Striker Ga'dok -> Commander Ri'mok -> Raigonn.

## 1. Saboteur Kip'tilak

**core**
1. Spread wide - the munitions and Sabotage both detonate in crosses
2. At 70% and 30% every munition on the floor goes off at once

**notes**
- `CD` healer - "the two mass detonations" - ability `Sabotage`

## 2. Striker Ga'dok

**core**
1. Read the emote for the Strafing Run direction and get to the safe side
2. Kill the adds before he lands, or the acid pools take the room

## 3. Commander Ri'mok

**core**
1. The tank kites him off the Viscous Fluid - standing in it stacks his damage
2. Stay out of the Frenzied Assault front

**notes**
- `KICK` need kick - "Bombard from the Krik'thik Saboteurs" - ability `Bombard`

## 4. Raigonn

**core**
1. Phase 1: hold the Krik'thik Protectorate waves while players take the launchers to the weak spot
2. Phase 2: the fixated player kites him away from everyone

**notes**
- (no role) - "Spread for Screeching Swarm, and stay out of the Engulfing Winds" - ability `Screeching Swarm`

---

## Second source, 2026-09-08

**Confirms:** Kip'tilak's bombs and the player debuff both detonating in fire
lines; the mass detonation at 70% and 30%; Ri'mok's Frenzied Assault frontal
and his Viscous Fluid pools; the Krik'thik Saboteurs' bombs.

**Adds names and detail:**
- The 70%/30% mass detonation is **World in Flames** — worth naming in the line.
- Viscous Fluid also *reduces player damage*, not only buffs his.
- Dodge the Saboteur bombs by moving diagonally.
